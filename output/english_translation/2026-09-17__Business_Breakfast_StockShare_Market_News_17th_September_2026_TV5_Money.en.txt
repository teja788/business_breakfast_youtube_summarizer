Oil and bond yields are transmitting that pressure into the economy. A 10-year yield close to 5% is not a small matter.

[Kranthi:] I travelled to Andhra and could not attend. On UPI MDR, UPI and NPCI played a major role in India's digitalization revolution during the last five to six years, especially after Covid. Discount brokers and platforms such as Upstox and Groww may be affected because customers transfer investment funds through them. The impact on capital-market companies can be direct. Each platform may have crores of account holders. Even if only 10,000 clients transfer Rs 10,000 apiece, the charge is material; quarterly settlement adds another flow of money from broker to client and back. Brokers are unlikely to absorb the entire expense indefinitely, so the ultimate consumer may bear it. Traditional transfer rails and different banking tie-ups could regain share. Consumer transactions have slabs and exemptions, but for investment platforms this is plainly a cost issue.
