[Kranthi:] Shares can be transferred. [Vasanth:] Suppose you transfer from your name to my name as a trusted account. What happens then?

[Kranthi:] There is complete clarity in taxation. If both demat accounts belong to the same person and the shares are moved from one to the other so that one account can be closed, there is no tax implication under the relevant provision of Section 47 of the Income-tax Act. A transfer between two different individuals has tax implications. Gift rules also differ between blood relatives and outsiders. For the same individual there is no capital-gains implication and no STT. STCG or LTCG does not arise merely from moving the holding between the same owner's accounts. An off-market transfer between different people must be handled carefully because it can also affect the recipient's future capital-gains treatment.
