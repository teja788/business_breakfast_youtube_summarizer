[Vasanth:] Good morning. A fresh trading week begins with most Asian markets negative, though Gift Nifty indicates an opening about 90 points higher because Brent crude eased from last week's high to roughly $92.7-92.8. The US has announced secondary economic sanctions against parties dealing with Iran, effectively targeting China and Russia. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent will explain the measures tonight. Iran's parliament has also approved a draft levy on ships using the Strait of Hormuz.

The US bond market remains the larger concern. The 30-year yield reached about 5.27% and the 10-year 4.73%. Yields have also risen globally: Japan 2.89%, UK 5.06%, Germany 3.26% and France 4.13%. US debt grew from $30 trillion to $40 trillion, and the world is watching the proposed bond-buying programme because it requires either more borrowing or money creation.

FIIs sold Rs 543 crore on Friday while DIIs bought Rs 2,124 crore. A 2.6% Lenskart block worth about $300 million was indicated near Rs 635, roughly a 4% discount. A Y Combinator-linked shareholder planned a Meesho block as well. Repeated large blocks and IPO supply absorb market liquidity, even when they transfer ownership from early to new investors.

The IPO market remains extremely active. TempSens Instruments had a grey-market premium around 100% and was described as an issue worth applying for. Lalithaa Jewellery, Horizon Industrial Parks and Fascinate Textiles were listing. Symbiotec Pharmalab's mainboard IPO was highlighted: it makes corticosteroid and steroid-hormone APIs, ranks among the global top ten and sought a valuation around Rs 6,000 crore.

[Kutumba Rao:] The market is likely to remain in consolidation. After eight or nine consecutive declines, the last two sessions produced a relief or dead-cat bounce. It becomes safer only above **24,400**; otherwise selling may re-emerge near that level because global cues remain poor. Banks have suddenly raised about $5 billion in foreign-currency loans, which needs monitoring, although India's macro fundamentals and balance of payments remain stronger than many peers.

There are strong Delhi rumours that an India-US trade deal is substantially complete and may be announced within a week after legal details. Watch US FOMC commentary, economic data, crude and bond yields. Gold should not be shorted; it reached about Rs 1.64 lakh for 24-carat and could ultimately move toward $5,000 an ounce. Bitcoin crossed $80,000, signalling speculative risk appetite.

For Nifty, first resistance is 24,300 and then 24,500. Support lies at 24,200 and 24,000. At 24,000, put open interest was roughly three times call OI; at 24,500, call OI was roughly 3.5 times put OI. Bank Nifty resistance was 58,500 and put support 57,000. Low VIX below 11 and PCR near 1.1 suggested confidence that the market could hold above 24,000-24,200. Historically, long eight-to-nine-session consolidations have often been followed by positive one-, three- and six-month returns. The September series could surprise positively, but investors should remain stock-specific.
