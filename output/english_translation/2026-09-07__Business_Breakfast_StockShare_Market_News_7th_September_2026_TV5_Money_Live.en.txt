US markets will not trade tonight. Another important development is the pre-open call-auction system. The pre-open session runs from 9:00 to 9:08 each day. From 9:00 to 9:05, participants can place market orders or limit orders. From 9:05 to 9:10, only limit orders are accepted. [Vasanth:] There is a small difference. Once the closing auction ends, nothing more can be done. If rigging occurs in the pre-open session, perhaps it can correct over the next half hour or one hour.

[Kutumba Rao:] The issue is not whether it corrects; it is that investors can be misled. Institutions and HNIs dominate perhaps 80%-90% of this market. I do not think allowing this in the pre-open session is correct. The existing system was smooth and there had been no problem. The call-auction system was introduced at the close because institutions used to prop up closing prices with last-minute buying.
