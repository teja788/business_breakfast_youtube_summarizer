Vasanth explained MTF. I covered the subject in an earlier Vijayawada presentation. Viewers often ask whether promoter pledging makes a stock weak. Promoter pledging can reveal promoter weakness, whether the money is used for the company or personally. But MTF pledging is entirely for the retail or HNI investor's own interest; the money does not go to the company.

Brokerages marketed MTF as easy money, inducing an investor who would normally buy 100 shares to buy 1,000 by offering more margin. MTF was a major reason for the mid- and small-cap rally. Its danger is now clear: after a 10-15% correction, brokers cut positions ruthlessly to recover their loans unless the investor replenishes margin. More forced selling may therefore appear in many stocks. Do not look only at promoter pledges. A high retail MTF position should itself be treated as a risk even when promoter pledging is zero.
