## Market view

- **Consolidation, not a runaway rally:** FII buying is restoring under-owned positions while DIIs sell. The next leadership may depend on where domestic institutions redeploy continuing SIP and mutual-fund inflows.
- **Macro caution:** GDP growth is strong, but unemployment at a six-quarter high deserves policy attention because weak job creation can create youth unease.
- **AI is correcting, not ending:** AI services are becoming indispensable like utilities. The key distinction is which companies can earn and manage capex; expected demand for the Anthropic IPO showed that optimism remains.
- **Levels and positioning:** **25,000** was the pivotal Nifty level, with highest call writing at **25,500** and put writing at **25,000**. A break below 25,000 could invite aggressive selling. Bank Nifty call open interest centered on **60,500**.
- **Follow rotation and volume:** He cited changing FPI interest across large-, mid-, small- and micro-cap names and urged investors to watch volume, results and sector rotation rather than cling to old themes.
