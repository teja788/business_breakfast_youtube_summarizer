## Market view

- **Consolidation was likely:** After three falling sessions and sharp small- and mid-cap corrections, even a stable session without a rise would be constructive.
- **Key downside levels:** **22,600** had become important, maximum put open interest was near **22,500**, and a breach of **22,500** would create additional downside risk. VIX had jumped to **15.76**, while PCR had slipped to about **0.85**; a move closer to **0.7** could offer a contrarian positional-buying opportunity.
- **Leverage remained dangerous:** Brokers were cutting positions, and stocks with **6-8%** MTF exposure had fallen **15-25%**. Investors should remain careful in small and mid caps.
- **Macro risks were building:** Brent near **$114**, an effective import cost of **$150-160** including freight and insurance, shrinking banking liquidity, cyber-security problems and US mid-term uncertainty could pressure the third quarter, especially November and December.
- **Stock-specific result season:** Investment opportunities depended on results, but rewards and punishments could both be severe. Stay invested selectively while maintaining caution.

- **Persistent Systems - Buy:** Hold existing shares and consider buying at current levels. Its European acquisition was ahead of its time, and Kutumba Rao expected the cloud-services company to perform well in the coming year despite a valuation above **50 times earnings**.
- **Siemens Energy India - Hold:** Retain existing shares, but do not buy afresh because AI-linked capital-equipment names could remain weak for some time.
- **Time Technoplast - Hold:** Hold through the upcoming result. The top line grew last quarter, though margins declined; reassess after the result.
- **MapmyIndia - Avoid:** Kutumba Rao rejected a fresh two-to-three-year holding case at the caller's entry level and described it as weak.
- **KPIT Technologies - Avoid:** Kutumba Rao likewise described it as weak at the caller's entry level and did not support the proposed two-to-three-year hold.
