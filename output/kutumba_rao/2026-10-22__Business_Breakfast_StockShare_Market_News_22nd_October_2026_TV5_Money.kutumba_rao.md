## Market view

- **Realignment, not panic:** The broad-market fall was removing froth from mid- and small-cap themes. Kutumba Rao said this could become a stock picker's paradise because selective markets reward careful company selection.
- **Leverage was the immediate risk:** MTF-driven buying had helped fuel the rally, and brokers would cut positions ruthlessly after **10-15%** corrections unless investors replenished margin. High retail MTF exposure should be treated as a risk even when promoter pledging is zero.
- **The difficult phase may last two to three months:** Investors should preserve capital, avoid weak speculative stocks, maintain cash and use falling prices to accumulate fundamentally sound companies gradually.
- **Potential catalysts:** A US trade deal could emerge in **one to three weeks**. The rupee had strengthened for eight consecutive days to about **94.47**, while Jio IPO inflows supported the last two sessions. Crude cooling would be another important positive.
- **Index and positioning:** The highest Nifty put interest was near **22,500**, and Kutumba Rao advised against fresh shorts after the break below **23,000**. Result season called for stock-specific decisions and very careful day trading.
