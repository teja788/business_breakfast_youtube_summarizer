## Market view

- **Keep positions small:** Expiry swings, weak rollovers, and another roughly **Rs 10,000 crore** of FII selling made this a market for low turnover and highly stock-specific positions.
- **Do not catch falling knives:** A stock can resume falling after a one-day pause; wait for a demonstrable bottom and use results rather than superficial stability.
- **Key levels:** **23,000** was major resistance, **22,800** the first hurdle, and **22,700** the important rollover-derived support. Put unwinding at **22,500** was also needed before the market could look healthier.
- **Possible temporary bottom:** If the second-half stability continued into a positive close, a short-term bottom might have formed; avoid new shorts and follow Q2 results carefully.
