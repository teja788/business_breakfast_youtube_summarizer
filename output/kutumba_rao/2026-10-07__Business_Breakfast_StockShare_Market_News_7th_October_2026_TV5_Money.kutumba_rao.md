## Market view

- **RBI largely discounted:** Yesterday's steady rebound and best one-day gain in roughly two months suggested that the policy decision might already be priced in, although the sudden **300-point Nifty / 2,000-point Sensex** cash-market swing was a volatility warning.
- **Hike versus growth:** Inflation was above the RBI's **4%** target, making a **0.25%** hike plausible, but the World Bank's **7.1%** growth forecast left room for a growth-supportive status quo.
- **Liquidity and external risks:** System surplus liquidity had fallen from about **Rs 12 lakh crore** to **Rs 4-6 lakh crore**. RBI commentary on crude above **$100**, high US yields and rupee pressure was therefore critical.
- **Corporate margin pressure:** Spot power rates had doubled and some thermal plants had only **7-10 days** of coal. Grid-dependent companies could see third-quarter margin pressure.
- **Extremely stock-specific market:** Expected Nifty-company revenue growth of about **20%** and profit growth of **22-23%** could eventually revive large caps, while resilient small and mid caps would increasingly depend on individual results.
- **Options/sentiment:** He identified **23,000-23,100** as resistance, puts at **22,500-22,700**, VIX at **13.6** after **14.5**, and said bulls would have firmer control only with VIX nearer **12**.

## Stock calls

- **Natco Pharma - Hold:** A viewer bought at **Rs 901** and the rights issue was discussed at **Rs 750**. Kutumba Rao said the current year could remain dull, improvement should become visible after February and at least another **six months** of patience was needed; he saw scope to return above four figures.
- **Waaree Energies - Watch:** For a viewer holding at **Rs 3,040**, he cited margin compression and the US tariff issue as the reasons for underperformance. BESS remained a preliminary opportunity, so he advised watching results for evidence that it had become the right time to average rather than giving an immediate add call.
