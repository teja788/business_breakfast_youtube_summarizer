## Market view
- **It is a sell-on-rallies market, not a buy-on-dips market.** "Nothing is surprising this morning, Vasanth garu, because I said it yesterday itself." He had flagged that the **weekend carries the tariff factor** and that any US decision would hang over the market as a shadow — "as expected, it has come." **Yesterday, for the first time in many days, we said this is not a buy-on-dips market, it is a sell-on-rallies market.** Anyone who **took fresh shorts intraday on a rally, or sold when the loss was only 30-40 points, can understand how happy they will be today.**
- **Levels**: yesterday was the **fourth successive down day**; today the **fifth**, opening gap-down. The question is whether **23,800 holds**. Below it, **23,600 is minor support**. "I have cautioned: **if 23,800 is breached with volumes, be prepared for 500 to 600 points** — it shouldn't be a surprise even if it falls all the way to **23,200 or 23,100**."
- **Macro is what is breaking, not the earnings.** Setting individual stocks and results aside, **macro factors turned weak unexpectedly**, so index counters and broader counters alike are going negative. **Until macro factors stabilise, "if you ask whether I think the market will recover fast — I have my very doubts."**
- **What to do**: the **results season is in full swing** and some companies that gave good results are **available at good valuations without having risen**, or where they rose the **rally was short-lived and they are correcting again**, giving **lower-level buying opportunities** — so **attempt stock picking**. **"But if you ask me, across-the-board buying is not advisable as of now — that must be understood clearly."** Trade with **extreme care**, **avoid weaker stocks**, **concentrate only on individual stocks** (where outperforming returns are still possible) and **stick to fundamentally sound stocks**.
- **A bubble is bursting in the US.** First it was **AI**, then the **chip trade**; IPOs were done on the back of it (**SK Hynix, SpaceX**). Now the same **borrowed/raised amounts**, plus **users complaining that token costs have risen**, have brought the **concern about whether these borrowings will pay off back on screen** — "we saw yesterday how **Tesla and Alphabet** were hammered."
- **No US rate cut.** One interesting factor: **weekly jobless claims came in at a multi-decade low, the lowest figure since 1969.** "When such a number comes, cutting rates will not happen" — whether US rates are cut **before this year-end can be placed under a big question mark; they may not be cut**. Asked whether next week's **Fed policy** could be hawkish: **"It will certainly be hawkish. After seeing this number, how else would they position it?"** Moreover **inflation is rising**, **crude has risen**, and **gasoline rates are rising across different US states**.
- **World economy**: because of **global connectivity**, a couple of bad decisions by Trump are impacting economies all over the world. The **World Bank now says the world economy may shrink this year — 2026, 2027, even on a calendar-year basis**; if such a **fatal shrinkage** comes, the impact won't be known today, it will show up **next year, in calendar 2027**.
- **Sectors**: **IT** rallied for a week and then corrected; after the Infosys result the **ADR traded 4-5% weak** [captions render "45%"] and **fresh losses may come today**. Where **refining and specialty chemical** companies gave good results the group rose — **Gandhar Oil, Savita Oil, Panama Petrochem** — but **whether that sustains has to be seen in the coming days**. Also watch **what steps SEBI takes in the cash market** and whether it will be a little liberal.
- **Flows — the number that can't be ignored**: **domestic institutions' purchases are the lowest in recent times**; **July DII buying is the lowest since April 2025** (in today's papers). Money may be going into **QIPs and IPOs**, but **in individual stocks, in the retail market, they are not buying**. Once **DII buying reduces the retail market becomes shallow**, and there is the possibility of **some stocks falling without volumes**.
- **The mid/small-cap rally is over.** Mid- and small-caps gave unexpected returns, but **the market discounted that in advance**: until **April the broader market was weak**, and it was only in **May, June and the first week of July** that many stocks rose **50 to 150%**. **"So the rally is complete in a way; therefore it is going into hibernation."** With **sector rotation, stock rotation and valuations shifting with global uncertainty, "it is going to be a tough exercise for investors."**
- **Options/positioning**: as said yesterday, **calls and puts accumulated right at 24,000** and **call writing at 24,000 was aggressive — that is being proved now**. Now **put options have increased a lot at 23,500**; **the 23,500 support must not be breached — if it is reached and breached there is grave danger for the market.** **PCR at 0.8.** **VIX rose again yesterday to near 13.5; above 15 is an alarming, dangerous level.** In **speculative longs the number of stocks, quantities and volumes are all falling sharply** — only **Eternal, Bajaj Holdings, Federal Bank and Manappuram** showed some. **Shorts increased in many stocks: Vishal Mega Mart, Mphasis, Nippon Asset Management, Adani Green, Bandhan Bank.** **Conviction delivery is in very few stocks — Bharti Airtel, Kotak Bank, Chola Finance and GMR Airports** (GMR Airports corrected 3% from its high, with some lower-level buying). **"All in all, it is time to be very cautious."**
- **Rupee**: it **held near 96.55** even on yesterday's weak day, but **if 97 is crossed in one stroke, fresh FII selling could emerge.**
- **Aviation / Adani**: the **Adani group has issued a clear denial that it is entering the airlines business**. So how **IndiGo / InterGlobe Aviation** reacts today matters — the **results came in weak but the announcement is some relief**. Still, "the **Adanis are a strong group**; the **Tatas could not achieve much, but the Adanis will disrupt the business**." On their track record — **they said the same about copper and then entered**. Airports is a big business so they will step carefully. **"If they do come it is good — in fact, in my view, they should come, because there is no competition; it has almost become a monopoly. Air India is there only in name. Only if a big player that beats IndiGo comes will the aviation sector's competitive environment be healthier."**

## Stock calls (viewer calls and mails)
- **Morepen Laboratories** *(caller Ashok; the anchor put the question to him — "can Morepen Labs be held, Kutumba Rao garu?")* — **Hold at current levels; don't sell.** The **company is only just now recovering**. But **all the on-and-off news about their subsidiary and the demerger is not playing out** — "the company **is doing pretty well as of now**, but only after all those play out can a bit of action come in it in the market." A **big mandate of about Rs 825 crore** came to them recently, under **commercial supplies**, and that has to be watched. **"Wait and watch. As of now I'd say don't sell; I say hold. Morepen — hold it."**
- **Vedanta demerged entities** *(Pavan's mail: 500 shares of each of the demerged companies, about Rs 62,000 in loss; the anchor named Kutumba Rao)* — **Analyse each stock separately; no blanket verdict.** "Each stock has to be analysed separately." If you wanted to treat all **five demerged companies as a block**, then on the **first listing day they gave nearly 20% returns** — cum-demerger to ex-demerger — and **one should have exited right then**. From here **power may behave one way, aluminium another way, the iron and steel business yet another way**, so **"look at the individual stock and take action."**
- **Lokesh Machines** *(Tejpal Reddy's mail: Rs 80 lakh invested, now at a 90% profit)* — **Book partial profits and put a trailing stop loss.** After the **American sanctions were removed** activity returned to the stock and there is **even a bit of hyper-activity**; but **if liquidity thins and it drops it will be either an upper circuit or a lower circuit**, because it has been **put into a 2% range — it is now in ESM**. So book partial profits and keep a trailing stop.

**Result estimate, not a call: Laurus Labs.** Asked by Sesha Sainath how the results (due today) would look, Kutumba Rao first said **"even if I had information I couldn't say — that's the problem."** Pressed for estimates he said: **"The result is fine, there is no doubt — 30% higher, quarter on quarter too"** (revenue), and in **profit a 30 to 40% upside**; and **"if the result is negative, there are no expectations in the market anyway."** This is a **numbers expectation with no buy/sell verdict**, so it is excluded from `buys.json`.
