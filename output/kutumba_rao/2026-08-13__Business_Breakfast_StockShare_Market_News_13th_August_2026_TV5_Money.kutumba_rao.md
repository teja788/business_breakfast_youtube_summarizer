**Kranthi (fundamental analyst):**
- **Tata Group succession** -- with Chandrasekaran's exit, **almost Rs 40,000 crore of wealth destruction** across the group yesterday. **Predictability is essential when a group commands the highest PE multiple**; the "who after February 2027" question creates **uncertainty**, and such uncertainty makes a **PE multiple contract severely**. On Noel Tata: a **regulation** stands in the way -- Tata Sons holds the group, Tata Trust holds Tata Sons, and Noel Tata is already Tata Trust chairman; **holding both parts is difficult**. Whether the regulation is changed or **new blood** is brought in is the uncertainty. Also **Shapoorji Pallonji**, one of the highest shareholders in Tata Sons, with the long-running **listing/corporate tussle** and the **Cyrus Mistry** precedent -- **"too many moving points."** These **fragile moments have been going on under the counter since Ratan Tata passed away**; Ratan Tata himself faced a tussle in **1991** succeeding JRD.
