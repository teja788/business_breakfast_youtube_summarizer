## F&O settlement time change (from Monday/August)
- **New pattern**: F&O stock futures and Nifty/index futures will have a **3:15 to 3:30 settlement window** with a matching mechanism. **Regular stocks close at 3:15** as before. The closing price from the new mechanism becomes the next-day opening price.
- This will **reduce income for brokers and HFT traders** (who exploit small arbitrage), but **exchange incomes won't be much affected**. More important for traders than investors.
