## Market view

- **Consolidation after the buffet:** He called the previous day's rally an unexpected buffet that investors needed time to digest. He expected consolidation, not a runaway rally.
- **IT carried the move:** Nifty held above **22,500**, crossed **22,600** and ran toward **22,800**. HCL Tech rose about **8.5%**, TCS **7%**, and Tech Mahindra and Infosys about **5%**. IT contributed the last **700-800 points**, so another sector would need to offset any IT profit-booking.
- **Domestic flows kept the market above 22,000:** More than **Rs 10 lakh crore** of mutual-fund assets were cited in mid- and small-cap stocks. Foreign investors had sold more than **Rs 3 lakh crore** in the current year, roughly double last year, but faith in India's growth story and mid/small-cap exuberance supported the market. The IMF raised India's FY27 outlook to **7%**.
- **Tax hopes remained conditional:** Nirmala Sitharaman's STCG/LTCG comments showed government concern, but later dilution by officials could reverse sentiment. He considered pro-market measures around the Budget possible, partly because Uttar Pradesh elections are due around **February**.
- **Derivatives turned positive very quickly:** Call writing shifted from **22,800** to **23,000**, with call positions also at **23,200**. Puts were heavy at **22,500** and building at **22,700**. PCR surged from **0.73** to **1.16** in one day, while VIX fell from above **15** toward **14**; he wanted VIX below **14** and nearer **12** for stronger bullish confidence.
- **Trade stock by stock:** Results season should produce news-driven action in Groww, Canara Bank, MRPL, Tata Technologies, Cyient, Elecon Engineering and other counters. He advised playing only on a stock-specific basis. He also flagged proposed **5% hydrogen blending** into piped natural gas as a possible future speculative theme.
