## Market view

- **Consolidation, not panic:** Monday's more-than-**250-point** correction was amplified by rumours. Sustained trade above **23,000** would keep the market in a safe zone, while failure to hold it could expose the high put-interest level near **22,500**.
- **Crude and currency were the main risks:** Crude had moved from **$90-95** to **$100-105** and then about **$110**. Its effect could be significant in the quarter ending December. RBI intervention kept the rupee below **95**, but it could return to **96-97** if support stops.
- **Flows still lacked conviction:** FIIs and domestic institutions bought on Friday, but FIIs sold almost **Rs 1,200 crore** on Monday. The put-call ratio was **0.93**, VIX about **14.828**, and Bank Nifty call interest concentrated near **56,000**.
- **Portfolio discipline:** Stay invested without panic, use stop-losses to protect trading profits, and use quarterly results as an opportunity to reshuffle portfolios. The US trade and refining headlines were positive, but had not yet produced measurable numbers.
