## Market view
- Q1 GDP growth was supported heavily by public investment: the central government had already used **37%** of its capex allocation in four months. Consumption fell and private capex remained softer than expected, showing that households and businesses were still conservative about global risks.
- Almost **22%** of cash-market turnover, nearly **Rs 40,000 crore**, occurred during the final half-hour of the MSCI rebalance. Such concentration can increase volatility fears among retail and smaller investors.
- FPIs had already invested almost **$5 billion** in IPOs this year. Robust primary-market flows could at some point cause a small secondary-market sell-off.
- Smallcaps, midcaps and the broader Nifty 500 were outperforming largecaps. Stay invested in fundamentally sound stocks, but check each company's story and earnings outlook rather than chasing activity.
- Nifty needed to cross **24,300** for a bullish formation. Call OI was highest at **24,500** and **24,300**, call writing was aggressive at **24,100**, and put concentration was at **24,000**. PCR had slipped to **0.91** and VIX risen to **11.19**, though it remained below 12.
- A close below **24,000** would require much greater caution. Crude, the rupee and eventual US rate increases remained the main global risks.
