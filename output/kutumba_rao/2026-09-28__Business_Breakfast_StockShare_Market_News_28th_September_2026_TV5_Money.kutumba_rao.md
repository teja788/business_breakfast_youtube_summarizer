Kutumba Rao was present and gave the market view and several stock-specific answers.

## Market view

- **Seven-week losing streak:** India was the BRICS underperformer at roughly **negative 11-12%**, despite much stronger markets in Brazil, China, and South Africa.
- **23,000 is decisive:** A sustained Nifty move below **23,000** could lead to another **400-500 points** of weakness; a recovery and firm close back above it could produce a short-term bounce.
- **Macro versus micro:** Crude, rupee weakness near **96**, geopolitical risk, and continuous FII selling made the macro picture cloudy, but improving balance sheets and earnings created select opportunities in small-, mid-, and microcaps.
- **Avoid volume traps:** Expensive or formerly inactive counters hitting repeated circuits should not be bought without verifying a fundamental change in the business story.

## Stock calls

- **Turtlemint - Buy:** The insurance-distribution selloff was overdone; hold existing shares and courageous investors could buy at current levels for a rebound.
- **Yatharth Hospitals - Sell:** Book partial profit after the strong rise and keep a trailing stop on the remainder because stake-sale news was already discounted.
