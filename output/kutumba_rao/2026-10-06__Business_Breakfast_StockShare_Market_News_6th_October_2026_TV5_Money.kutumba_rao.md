## Market view

- **Relief, not full confidence:** Nifty recovered from negative territory and closed roughly **100-130 points** higher despite weakness in HDFC Bank and IT, while about **13 of 16 sectors** closed positive.
- **Market at a crossroads:** Heavy domestic liquidity was offset by high crude, elevated bond yields, European stress, rupee weakness and broader global political risks. At least one sector needed to lead during results season for the move to become sustainable.
- **Dead-cat-bounce risk:** Crude above **$100** and reliance on FCNR rather than durable capital meant the rebound could still fail.
- **Options/sentiment:** He placed resistance at **22,800**, treated **22,500** as the key put and caution zone, noted PCR's move from about **0.7** toward **0.94-1**, and warned that VIX near **14.7** could trigger profit-booking if it sustained above **15**.
- **Results-led positioning:** Q2 visibility was already producing stock-specific moves. He highlighted Tata Technologies-Honda, Kotak Mahindra Bank and AWL Agri, while urging investors to concentrate on company updates rather than assume Wall Street gains would automatically carry India higher.

## Stock calls

- **Amara Raja - Hold:** The Telangana expansion and Chinese battery-technology restrictions could delay progress, but he saw no fundamental crisis and said the company was adapting. Patience was required.
- **Delhivery - Accumulate:** Institutional participation had risen, the company was a major Amazon logistics partner, and the stock was stabilising near **Rs 400** after correcting from above **Rs 500**.
- **Urban Company - Avoid:** Do not buy until the company becomes profitable; after its sharp rebound from lower levels, he would not enter immediately.
- **Shadowfax - Buy/Prefer:** He preferred Shadowfax as a logistics alternative because of its innovative business model and exclusive partnerships, including Meesho.
- **Olectra Greentech - Hold:** Industry and business-model churn, including greater use of leasing, required patience, but he expected the company to stabilise.
