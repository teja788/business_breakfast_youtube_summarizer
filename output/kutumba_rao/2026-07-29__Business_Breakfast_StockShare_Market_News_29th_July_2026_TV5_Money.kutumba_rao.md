## Market view
- **The August series goes into consolidation mode — no runaway rally.** "If there were any runaway rally here, I would say it would be a big surprise."
- **Rollover data was very subdued** — market players are not enthusiastic. Two reasons: in July, even though **mid-cap/small-cap activity increased**, the **average daily cash-segment turnover fell to nearly a seven-month low**, and **derivative turnover had its biggest fall in five months**.
- **Regulatory drag**: a month ago the **bank-guarantee rules were changed** (by the regulators [as heard, "CBRLs"] or the exchanges) to require **100% collateral instead of the earlier 50%**, so **maintaining trading positions is harder and costlier for brokers**. These restrictions will continue, and the **results season is almost over**.
- **Levels**: unless exceptional macro news arrives, he does not expect a sharp rise. **"We should be lucky if the Nifty opens above 24,000 today and sustains 24,000, with the new base at 23,800."** Intra-month, **an attempt at 24,000 and a move up to 24,800 [as heard] should already be counted as lucky**; otherwise it stays inside the consolidation band.
- **Only two things can break the band**: (1) the **US Fed**, and (2) the **West Asian geopolitical issue** — whether a deal draws the curtain on it once and for all. **The war is spreading; even some Gulf states are starting to attack Iran.** If it isn't ended and a larger issue comes on screen, **markets can lose more**. "Let us assume — and hope — that they put a full stop to it."
- **Macro is genuinely positive**: **manufacturing data at a 23-month high** ("highly positive"), on top of **IIP at a multi-month high** last month. The economy is **very resilient** and **better than other nations**, and **strong despite the geopolitical factors**.
- **IT is now the market's defensive support.** The sector that underperformed for the last couple of weeks has begun to hold the index up. Even with the **FMCG sell-off led by Hindustan Unilever** (only a moderately average result, yet the stock corrected and dragged the index) and with **private banks as non-performers**, "we see the index standing only because of IT stocks." **A prolonged pessimism has ended.** He had written **"buy IT stocks"** as a **defensive bet** in his newspaper column (the Monday before last): value was clear, because each of them holds **Rs 30,000-40,000 crore of cash reserves** — Infosys, TCS and the rest — so **they can reinvent themselves post results**.
- **Global rotation supports traditional IT**: **Apple has gone to $5 trillion** while **Nvidia has come from $5.7 trillion down to $4.77 trillion**. The sell-off around **AI-spending concerns** and **tokenisation concerns** is "maybe beneficial for the traditional IT sector — hopefully it continues."
- **Pharma** has been an outperformer for the last few days.
- **Infra is turning**: L&T's result was **"extremely good"** — order inflow up, a **~Rs 15 lakh crore project pipeline**, Q1 **up more than 14%**. If order books keep growing and **government infrastructure spending increases**, the **infra stocks that have underperformed until now will perform better**. Not an immediate jump, but **slowly, from here, the possibility of a rise is clearly visible**. **Defence and the other new sectors are anyway positive**, and the **AI benefits** are visible in new stocks coming on screen and playing out positively.
- **Options/positioning**: rollovers subdued, reflected in yesterday's index move of only **30-40 points** — a very narrow band after many days. **Call OI: 24,000 weekly, 24,500 monthly. Put OI: 24,000 weekly; 23,700 and 23,500 on the monthly** — so **if any weakness sets in, 23,500 could be attempted this time**. **PCR declined modestly to 1.05** (only consolidation, closer to one). **VIX at 12.56** and weak — **in favour of the bulls**. Speculative longs are not worth considering because yesterday was rollover day. **Shorts after weak results: Godfrey Phillips, Suzlon, DMart.** **Delivery: Sun Pharma, Bharti Airtel, Uno Minda, NTPC.** **Highest rollovers (97-98%) only in "stuck" stocks** where retail is trapped — **Godfrey Phillips, Crompton, Fortis, Godrej Consumer, Jio Finance** — of **no great significance**; "the stuck players rolled over without booking losses."
- **Stock-specific activity will still continue.** Post results, **Rossell Techsys corrected more than 7%**; **Usha Martin** had huge volumes but rose only **2-3%**; **Aurionpro Solutions**, a somewhat speculative mid-cap IT stock, **fell 12%** even while other mid-cap IT performed well.
- **Results due today that he flagged for careful observation** (agenda items, not calls): **Adani Enterprises** and **Adani Ports** — the major news being that **Adani Ports may participate in the British ports takeover bid**; if it lands, **"it will become a global player," among the top five or six in the world after Maersk and Dubai Ports** (Adani Enterprises also recently did a QIP). Also **Colgate-Palmolive** (after HUL, to read FMCG), **MTAR Technologies** (the stock went to a high and is in corrective mode — will it deliver in line with expectations), **Piramal Pharma** (a long-time underperformer but **expanding well in CDMO**, so after Laurus's results **it can give a better result**), **Blackbuck**, **Dabur India**, and the three recently demerged **Vedanta Power / Vedanta Iron / Vedanta Oil** entities.
- **A Guru Purnima reflection**: circumstances change fast, so **"I have to change my views according to the changing environment."** One should not dig in as though under a vow — "we hold conviction in a stock at one point in time; when we come to know it is going to fail, we must change our stance," otherwise the loss is our own.
