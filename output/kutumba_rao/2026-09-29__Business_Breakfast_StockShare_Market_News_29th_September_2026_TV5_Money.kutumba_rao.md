## Market view

- **Downside zone:** A decisive break below **23,000** made a test of **22,000-22,500** unsurprising; such a test could eventually offer a buying opportunity.
- **Risk-off regime:** Falling gold and Bitcoin alongside expensive crude showed broad global risk aversion. Occasional dead-cat bounces should be used to exit weak stocks, not for indiscriminate bottom fishing.
- **Buy value, avoid crowding:** Selected pharma, aerospace, defence, capital-goods, financial, and consumption names could offer value, but expensive smallcaps with weak cash generation or dependence on repeated equity issuance should be avoided.
- **Wait for earnings:** India's IIP remained good, so company-level opportunities persisted, but the weak market undercurrent, Tata-group overhang, **7.19%** Indian ten-year yield, RBI policy, and earnings argued for small positions and patience.
