## Market view

- **RBI**: with this status quo, the **next meeting will maintain the status quo** — **neither a cut nor a hike**. **"The only thing is that the commentary will become key."**
- **The US Fed decision is crucial**, not only for global markets but for ours, because **FII flows are by and large dictated by the Fed**.
- **The chip crash is no surprise to him.** He had flagged **last week** that after the chip run-up the trade becomes a **sell-on-rallies trade — "it is not a buy-on-dips rally."** **SanDisk peaked above 2,000 and has come closer to 1,200**; how big that percentage loss is and whether it enters **bear-market territory** must be watched. Chip valuations are **fizzling out**, so the Korean market falling and Nvidia/SanDisk falling in the US is "not at all surprising."

Initial numbers are **"not too bullish but positive"** and **equivalent to the 2013 issue's numbers**; **$60-70 billion** could be closed. If the issue is **successful**, there is a chance the **rupee settles down** and the **markets take it slightly positively**.

Signs that IT will act as a **defensive bet for the index** are "clearly visible."
- **Banks**: **FII exposure is being increased in PSU banks and reduced in private banks**; in public sector banks the **NPAs and net interest income are decent**.
