## Market view

- **Breakout from consolidation:** The market appeared to have moved beyond the **22,500-23,000** band. Defending **23,000** on the downside could keep **23,500** on track, although consolidation was still possible.
- **Domestic economy remained resilient:** Record GST growth and a GDP reading around **7.8-8%** supported the view that India is among the world's most resilient economies. Q2 earnings were broadly on track and mostly positive.
- **Inflation was the key domestic risk:** Consumer inflation near **5.54** and food inflation around **7.74**, attributed partly to El Nino, were the main concern. PMI and whether FIIs continued buying after nearly **nine weeks** would be watched.
- **Global risks remained material:** West Asia, the **November 3** US midterms, and about **$700 billion** of borrowings by four large technology companies could move global markets.
- **Derivatives supported bulls:** Call build-up around **23,500**, PCR near **1.4**, and VIX around **15** supported a positive bias. A move in VIX below **15** would be still more favorable, and the broad advice was to remain invested.

## Stock calls

- **Laurus Labs - Hold:** A long-term investor sitting on a **50%** profit need not sell or hurry to book profit. Short-term positional traders could manage the position after a normal **4-5%** correction from the result peak; the stock had moved from about **Rs 2,440** to **Rs 2,390** that morning.

Kutumba Rao said there was nothing to worry about as of now, but the stock has sharp swings; minor support was around **Rs 460** and major support around **Rs 397-425**, versus a current price near **Rs 495**.
