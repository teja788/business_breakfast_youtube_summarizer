## Market view

- **Consolidation remained likely:** Nifty needed to recover and defend **22,700**. Sustained trade below that level could revisit **22,500** and confirm weakness.
- **Expiry framework:** Nifty support was near **22,500** and resistance near **22,900**. Bank Nifty support was near **53,500**, with resistance near **54,800**.
- **Volatility and macro cues stayed negative:** VIX had approached **18**, PCR was not supportive, the rupee closed near **95.15**, and oil around **$109-110** remained an obstacle to a significant rally.
- **Treat the rebound selectively:** Mid- and small-cap interest returned, but one day's recovery should not be mistaken for renewed post-April exuberance. Trade stock-specifically and sector-specifically and pay for quality and earnings.
- **Results must drive decisions:** Stay invested when results are good and momentum confirms them; exit companies with weak results. Watch expiry rollovers because sectors with stronger rollovers could contain the winners.
