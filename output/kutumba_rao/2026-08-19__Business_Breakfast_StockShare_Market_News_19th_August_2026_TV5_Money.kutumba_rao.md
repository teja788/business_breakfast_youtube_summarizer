## Market view
- Market is in **consolidation mode**, not definitively weak. **24,200 is the key support** -- it was breached yesterday (closed at 24,154), which is "a big negative." To escape this negative, the market must conclusively stand in the **24,200-24,400 band** and cross it -- which "looks somewhat difficult."
- **"Today is the most important day."** If the market does not recover from lower levels today, it will become a **sell-on-rallies market**, at least in the short term. Market is stuck in an **extremely narrow band of 24,000 to 24,500**.
- **No runaway rally possible** while so many geopolitical cues are negative. Only **stock-specific, sector-specific rotation-driven rallies** will continue.
- If **Iran goes to a fully offensive military posture**, a **sharp correction from current levels cannot be ruled out**. Higher crude oil prices are definitively negative for India (big importer). Multi-decade high bond yields will also indirectly impact markets.
