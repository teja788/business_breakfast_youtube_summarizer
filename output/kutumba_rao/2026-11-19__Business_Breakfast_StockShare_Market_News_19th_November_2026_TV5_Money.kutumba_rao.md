## Market view

- **Healthy consolidation:** Some consolidation is necessary after the recent rally. The correction was not driven by FII selling; FIIs bought more than **Rs 5,000 crore**, nearly **Rs 6,000 crore**, while domestic institutions sold about **Rs 3,000 crore** on the correction day.
- **Foreign buying supports India:** November FPI/FII purchases were nearing or exceeding **Rs 1 lakh crore**, and Goldman Sachs upgraded India to Overweight with a Nifty target near **28,500**.
- **Buy on dips:** Every correction is a buying opportunity. Even a roughly **400-point Nifty correction** should be treated as an opportunity to buy, not sell, provided investors remain extremely selective.
- **Key index levels:** **25,000** is emerging as major Nifty support and **25,500** remains the main options barrier. Bank stocks remain attractive on corrections because valuations still resemble their 2024 levels.
- **Risks:** More than **Rs 1.5 lakh crore** is deployed through MTF, so any SEBI restriction linked to free float could force unwinding in mid- and small-caps. Rupee strength may also challenge exporters even though it signals strong economic fundamentals.
