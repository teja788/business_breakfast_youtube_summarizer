Kutumba Rao was present and gave the opening market view and several stock-specific answers.

## Market view

- **Broader-market complacency:** Strong volume in suddenly active small and midcaps may not persist. If institutions return to frontline names or IPO activity weakens, speculative broader-market stocks can be dumped quickly.
- **Accumulation zone:** He treated **23,300-23,500** as a gradual accumulation area for medium- and long-term positional investors, while Nifty must regain **23,500** and a move toward **23,100** would be dangerous.
- **Do not short aggressively:** With PCR near **0.7**, VIX at **11.9**, and prolonged selling already visible, aggressive fresh shorts at current levels were not advisable.
- **Macro caution:** A rupee persistently above **95** and crude above **$100** could worsen inflation. He favored small, staged purchases rather than deploying all capital at once.
