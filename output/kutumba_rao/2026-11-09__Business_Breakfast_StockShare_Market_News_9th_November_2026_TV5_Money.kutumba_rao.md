## Market view

- **Consolidation before another rally:** After one of the largest weekly gains in roughly six years, he wanted the market to digest gains and build a base. A continued unchecked rise could invite a sharp news-driven correction.
- **Earnings over random picking:** Most Q2 results should be out by the **15th**. He called this a time for homework and preferred companies whose earnings justify their moves.
- **India is not cheap:** He cited MSCI India's forward P/E at **18.45** versus **8.84** for Asia excluding Japan. Foreign buying above **Rs 45,000 crore** favored large caps, but investors should watch whether it survives the valuation reset.
- **Buy on declines with care:** Pharma, metals and cement were relatively strong, but he urged investors not to rush. He described the market as buy-on-dips while stressing results and portfolio allocation.
- **Data-center concerns deserve answers:** India has about **1.5 GW** of data-center capacity and may reach **10 GW by 2030**. He expected less public resistance than in the US, but said governments and companies must address real power, water, noise and environmental questions.
