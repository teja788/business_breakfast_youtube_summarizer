## Market view

- **A better week was possible after five consecutive declines**, but rising inflation, crude near **$106-107**, high global bond yields, and persistent FII selling meant caution was still essential.
- **Key Nifty levels:** Downside support was **23,000-23,100**; immediate resistance **23,600-23,700**. Calls were concentrated at **24,000/23,700**, puts at **23,300**, and PCR near one.
- **Avoid leverage:** FIIs sold more than **Rs 11,000 crore** on Friday while DII buying of roughly **Rs 1,500-2,000 crore** failed to change the trend. Risk-management discipline was more important than aggressive positioning.
- **Selective leadership:** Pharma and health care remained strong; IT and realty could see only a dead-cat bounce.
