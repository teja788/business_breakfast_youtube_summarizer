## Market view

- **Expected correction, critical support:** He had expected a **250-300 point** cut and was not surprised by the move beyond **400 points**. He expected **22,000** to hold over the medium term, but warned of difficult conditions if it failed.
- **Earnings could cushion the market:** TCS's approximately **$10 billion** of second-quarter deal wins and consistent business mix were positive. Nifty 50 Q2 earnings growth was estimated at **24.7%**, potentially the best in **12 quarters**.
- **Global flows dominated:** Continued FII selling was unsurprising while the rupee remained weak near **96.88**. GQG's sale was part of a broader global-investor flow issue rather than a domestic-fundamental shock.
- **Technical pressure:** VIX rose above **15** to about **15.275**, favouring bears. PCR near **0.73** entered a contrarian buying zone, while call concentration at **22,500** and **22,700** made the first of those levels difficult resistance.
- **Selective positioning:** He expected a possible pullback toward **22,350-22,400**, preferred defensive pharma, active metals and precision engineering, and advised patient investors to use Q2 to reshuffle portfolios from weak to quality stocks.
