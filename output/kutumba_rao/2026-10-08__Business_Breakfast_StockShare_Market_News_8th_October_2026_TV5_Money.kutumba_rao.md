## Market view

- **Hawkish policy pressure:** The expected **25-basis-point** RBI hike arrived, but hawkish commentary disappointed the market. With the rupee near **96.77-96.8**, he saw no clear large-cap or benchmark recovery while depreciation continued.
- **Cloudier macro outlook:** The dollar was at an **18-month high**, another US rate hike by December remained possible, and higher domestic rates would raise EMIs and other costs.
- **Institutional imbalance:** FIIs sold more than **Rs 6,000 crore** while DIIs bought about **Rs 4,500 crore**. He warned that weak December mutual-fund return reports could slow domestic inflows.
- **Technical caution:** Call writing was heavy at **23,000**, **22,700** and **22,600**; **22,500** was major support. PCR fell from **1.06** to about **0.85**, VIX was near **14**, and some established chartists did not rule out **20,000-20,500** over three to six months.
- **Stock-specific results season:** Newer stocks and sectors could outperform while Nifty-heavy names remained weak, but investors should avoid indiscriminate buying and decide positions from results.

## Stock calls

- **Vodafone Idea - Hold:** For a caller holding at **Rs 14**, keep only a limited position because losses remain high. Customer additions and restructuring have held the stock around **Rs 13-15**; safety exists while it remains above **Rs 13**.
- **Voltamp Transformers - Hold:** In a comparison with newly listed Kanohar Electricals, he preferred staying with established Voltamp until Kanohar builds one or two quarters of market history.
- **Natco Pharma - Add:** A holder of **700 shares at Rs 860** should apply for the rights issue to lower the average cost, then wait; Kutumba Rao saw scope for a good profit next year.
- **MosChip Technologies - Watch:** He did not expect spectacular returns and noted that recent rallies had fizzled. The stock had safety above **Rs 180**, but a break below **Rs 180** could lead to new lows.
