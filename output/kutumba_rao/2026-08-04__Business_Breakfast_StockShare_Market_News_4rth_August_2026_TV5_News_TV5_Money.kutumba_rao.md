## Market view
- **Closing Auction Session (CAS) is "unwarranted confusion" and "a big faux pas" by regulators.** The first-day CAS created a ~200-point pricing mismatch in the Nifty (official close 24,774 vs. true close ~24,554). NSE auction volume was only Rs 1,200 crores and BSE just Rs 10 crores -- in such a narrow setup, the distortion was inevitable. He used the Telugu idiom: "the fox got burnt imitating the tiger" -- meaning India blindly copied NYSE/LSE without adaptation. He noted that even **Nasdaq has no CAS system** despite being the most important exchange. With India's heavy speculative activity and cash settlement, regulators should have been ultra-cautious. If the confusion is not resolved, **it could itself trigger a downtrend**. Today being a **settlement day**, any repeat confusion could cause settlement headaches that "nobody can predict."
- **LIC OFS is "very surprising and a big negative for the market."** Rs 31,000 crores being sucked out at one go locks up institutional money that could have gone to the secondary market or other IPOs. Institutions will subscribe -- whether by government order or "long term anyway won't be a loss" reasoning -- but the opportunity cost is real.
- **Wait-and-watch attitude expected** because tomorrow is the **RBI policy meeting**. Status quo is expected, but the **commentary is critical** -- on inflation, the vegetable crisis, and notably the impact of the FCNR(B) option, which has caused FII investment in Indian treasury instruments to drop to **near zero**. "On one side they say we're bringing funds through forex, but here the debt market is drying up." Whether RBI comments on this bears watching.
- **August series is going to be a positive series** -- said this from day one. FIIs bought Rs 20,000 crores in July and buying continues into August. They have also **covered ~25% of their short positions** (from 2.06 lakh to ~1.56 lakh contracts). This is a noteworthy positive.
- **Options data / positioning**: Support at **24,400-24,500** (put options have moved to 24,500 with highest OI at 24,500-24,600). Resistance around **24,600-24,800** (call options piled up at 24,600; call writing aggressive at 24,800). If confusion is overcome in the next couple of days, **25,000 can be attempted this series** -- monthly positions are building there. Only below 24,400-24,500 should one be worried.
- **PCR stable at 1.4** -- bulls are confident. **VIX modest increase, still below 12** -- bulls in control. After very long, **200-300% single-day returns** appeared in weekly options (24,500, 24,600, 24,700 strikes all gave 220-240%) -- out-of-the-money option interest will grow again.
