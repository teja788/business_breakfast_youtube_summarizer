## Market view

- **Complacency is the main risk.** Large caps and the headline indices remain weak while midcaps and small caps are near all-time highs after roughly **20-30%** gains from their March-April lows. That exuberance is seasonal and cannot continue indefinitely; investors must separate durable companies from story-driven disasters.
- **Nifty versus the broader market:** He described the Nifty as already in a bear market but said the resilient midcap and small-cap indices keep the overall broader market in bullish territory. The market has split in two.
- **Critical levels:** A downside breach of **23,500** could reveal real bearishness; call writing was already visible near **23,800**. Investors should abandon complacency and remain cautious.
- **Liquidity pressure:** IPOs, including the NSE offer expected to open on the **18th** and a possible Jio issue, may divert money from the secondary market and weaken follow-up buying.
- **Leadership:** Pharma and selected innovative mid-/small-cap companies with earnings visibility remain stronger, but the season will eventually expose weak businesses. He cited new highs in Neuland Labs, Laurus Labs, Divi's Laboratories, Sai Life Sciences, Solar Industries, and Jyoti CNC Automation, while several insurers and large consumer/financial names were near 52-week lows.
