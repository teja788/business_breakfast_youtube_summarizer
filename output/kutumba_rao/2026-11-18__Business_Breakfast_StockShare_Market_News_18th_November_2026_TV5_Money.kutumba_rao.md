## Market view

- **Rally can sustain after consolidation:** Nifty must cross and hold **25,500** for a fresh leg; if it stays above that level for a couple of days, an all-time high is likely before year-end.
- **Large caps and banks lead:** Reliance, Adani Enterprises, HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank are forming new bases. Bank Nifty has gained about **11.5%** this month while its valuations remain below October 2024 levels, so bank stocks are attractive on declines.
- **Flows and currency are supportive:** FIIs have bought almost **$10 billion** in November, and the rupee's roughly **75-paise** two-day move was its strongest in about **20 months**.
- **Risks to watch:** Rising promoter pledges, leveraged MTF activity in mid/small caps, gold-loan stress, an AI correction after Anthropic's IPO, and the continuing **25,500** options barrier.
