Kutumba Rao was present and gave the main market view plus seven conservatively attributable stock calls.

## Market view

- **Consolidation after a four-month-best session:** The high-volume large-cap rally showed improving institutional sentiment. Support could emerge around **22,600-22,700**, making the market buy-on-dips at least until Diwali.
- **Macro receipts were strong:** Direct-tax collections rose **13.2%**. Six-month STT collections reached **Rs 46,840 crore**, versus about **Rs 31,000 crore** a year earlier and the captioned **63%** comparison with the budget estimate. Rural unemployment was about **4.2%** and urban unemployment **6.9%**.
- **Results required discipline:** Nestle India fell almost **8%**, and BHEL and Canara Bank about **6-6.5%**, illustrating the need to watch each result and management commentary rather than trade the whole market indiscriminately.
- **Flows and currency improved:** The rupee strengthened for a fifth session and closed around **94.99**. FIIs sold roughly **Rs 5,200 crore**, while domestic institutions bought about **Rs 3,300 crore**. FDI activity continued to demonstrate overseas confidence in India.

## Stock calls

- **Yes Bank - Avoid:** The Supreme Court AT1-bond decision caused a roughly **15%** fall. Kutumba Rao reiterated his long-standing advice to avoid the stock.
- **State Bank of India - Buy/prefer:** He again advised shifting from Yes Bank into SBI as the stronger alternative.
- **Agastya Energy - Watch:** Recent activity and pending results could produce a surprise; watch carefully rather than chase.
- **Premier Energies - Watch:** Its Andhra Pradesh unit had launched, and Kutumba Rao said Premier and Agastya could surprise in some manner.
- **Sigma Advanced Systems - Watch:** Positive announcements, including reported Rolls-Royce orders, were not enough to remove execution risk. The caller bought high; the stock could fall further, so wait for one or two months/quarters and management commentary.
- **Yatharth Hospital - Watch:** It was stable and could rise slowly after promoter divestment, but exceptional returns should not be expected.
- **Max Healthcare - Avoid:** An old Ranbaxy-related court case remained an overhang. Kutumba Rao advised avoiding it until concrete details emerge.
