Kutumba Rao was present and gave both the opening market view and several clearly addressed Q&A recommendations.

## Market view

- The failure to sustain a positive open and an **85-point** Nifty loss were disappointing, especially with Brent below **$100** and the rupee near **95.62**.
- The heaviest IPO rush in **30 years**, including potentially more than **Rs 1 lakh crore** tied up if NSE was subscribed five times, was draining secondary-market liquidity.
- FIIs sold close to **Rs 4,000 crore** while domestic institutions bought more than **Rs 4,000 crore**. VIX at **10.99** implied a range-bound market rather than an aggressive selloff.
- He favored extremely stock-specific positional trades in fundamentally sound companies ahead of Q2 results and highlighted exchange-source data through NSE Lens.

## Stock calls

- **NSE - Hold:** Long-term investors should hold after listing regardless of an immediate premium or discount; NSE has close to **80%** market share and no true domestic comparison.
- **Lalithaa Jewellery - Hold:** Hold for the long term because top-line growth continued and post-listing volatility has stabilized despite subdued net profit.
- **Avanti Feeds - Hold:** Hold the Rs 870 position through potentially difficult tariff-affected quarters; a slightly better result may support a move toward roughly **Rs 970**.
- **Havells - Hold:** Retain the dull, range-bound stock near its 52-week low because brand equity remains strong.
- **Mold-Tek Packaging - Buy:** Existing holders may retain it and fresh investors can buy in weakness, while noting inconsistent management execution.
- **Ganesh Ecosphere - Hold:** The circular-economy/recycling business is performing well; its market capitalization has grown from roughly **Rs 1,000 crore to Rs 2,700 crore**.
