## Market view

- **Recovery could be durable:** The **237-point** Nifty rally was broad-based across realty, FMCG, selected banks, PSUs, mid caps and small caps. The market may consolidate to digest gains, but sustained buying suggested the recovery could hold.
- **Key Nifty levels:** Put interest shifted to **22,700**, making it major support. Nifty needed to cross and hold **23,000**; call interest was concentrated at **23,500**, then **23,400-23,300**, consistent with his earlier **23,500** series target.
- **Sentiment improved but could overheat:** PCR jumped to **1.31**; around **1.4-1.5** could invite profit booking. VIX fell by about one percentage point toward **15**, while the **13-14** zone would be more comfortable.
- **Global and weekend caution:** Crude near **$110-115** and the weekend geopolitical factor remained risks. Raw-material pressure was already visible in Maruti and Hyundai margins, while the US AI selloff and European rate outlook required monitoring.
- **Buy-on-declines bias:** The market was slowly turning into a buy-on-dips setup, but today's bellwether results could swing it. Fresh Nifty positions were advised only after a sustained move above **23,000**.

## Stock calls

- **TempSense Instruments India - Watch:** It is a good company but, in Kutumba Rao's view, an overhyped niche story with competing products. Wait for the September-quarter result and reassess on Monday rather than reacting to a move from the caller's **Rs 550** purchase to about **Rs 514**.
- **Titagarh Rail Systems - Sell:** Railway orders may generate a relief rally, but the old glory days have not returned and a valuation near **40 PE** is high for a wagon manufacturer. Investors stuck at higher prices should use a move toward **Rs 750-800** to exit.
- **Trent - Buy:** After a strong result reaction of roughly **5.5-6%**, an intraday decline could provide an opportunity to buy.
- **Sun Pharma - Buy:** If intraday weakness brings the share toward **Rs 1,750-1,760**, it can be bought; it had returned to action with a gain above **2%** after a prolonged dull period.
