- The **$127 billion** FCNR inflow is positive now, but the three-year repayment liability may merely postpone the currency problem. The rupee's level at maturity will determine whether the scheme was truly successful.
- India's upgrade from **BBB+ to A-** should gradually reduce overseas borrowing costs. Banking-system liquidity is at a roughly four-year high of **Rs 7.77 lakh crore**, although gold imports rising **47%** can drain forex.
- Nifty needs to recover and hold above **24,000**. Highest call open interest is at **24,200**, put activity is increasing near **23,800**, PCR is around **0.87**, and VIX near **11.6** can be ignored only while it remains below **12**.
- Roughly **Rs 6,000 crore** of anchor-investor shares will exit lock-in this month. Expect stock-specific action and watch how newly listed shares sustain after those releases.
- Remain invested and do not panic-sell. Participation, volume and breadth are gradually improving, but speculative positions and illiquid counters require caution.
