## Market view
- Intraday rallies have repeatedly fizzled over the last **four sessions** because uncertainty is preventing follow-up buying, especially in large caps. Activity is shifting toward newer cash-market and Nifty 500 names.
- More than **$120 billion** of deposits have created abundant liquidity, but credit is still offered at high rates. The RBI's next MPC meeting and its treatment of liquidity, interest rates and the rupee are more important domestically than US commentary unless the Fed actually raises rates.
- The rupee reached a **ten-week high**, but investors must see whether that strength persists. GST collections have returned toward **Rs 2 lakh crore**, led by Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu, while Telangana and Andhra Pradesh lag.
- UltraTech's cable entry is partly digested and should hurt weaker, highly indebted cable companies most. NSE's IPO clearance makes it a matter of weeks, but unlisted-share trades around **Rs 1,945-Rs 2,045** remain risky.
- Watch FII flows: a sustained Nifty move above **24,000** and then **24,500** could open a path toward new highs. Put buildup is concentrated at **23,500**, PCR is near **0.8**, VIX is around **11.33**, and crude near **$96** is a danger signal.
- Small players should avoid options because closing-auction volatility and premium evaporation favour only participants able to absorb large swings. Positional investors can stay mildly bullish and remain invested.
