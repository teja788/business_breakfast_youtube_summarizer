`output/scorecard/` which scores the **real** analyst's calls mined from episode
transcripts. Logged so bot calls can be marked to market later.

Format: one section per session. Price = quote at the time of the call (Yahoo,
intraday). Verbs are from Kutumba's set: Buy / Add / Accumulate / Hold / Avoid /
Sell / Watch.
