## Global backdrop

- **Crude remained the main risk:** Brent traded above **$110** amid the continuing West Asian crisis. Pakistan was again trying to mediate between Iran and the US, while Marco Rubio said an agreement with the Iranian regime would be difficult.
- **Bond and European credit pressure persisted:** The US 10-year yield was about **5.11%**. The German-French sovereign spread reached a recent high of **156 basis points**, reviving concern about a European credit crisis amid unrest in France.
- **US refining needs supported India:** Rubio's Jamnagar visit highlighted Reliance's ability to process Venezuelan heavy crude while war has disrupted refineries in the Gulf and Europe. The discussion framed India as a refining superpower and a possible answer to the US diesel shortage.

## Indian markets

- **Monday's sell-off was retail-led:** Roughly **Rs 5 lakh crore** of market capitalisation was erased. DIIs bought about **Rs 3,200 crore** and FIIs sold **Rs 1,149 crore**, yet Nifty fell about **1.1%**; breadth was only **700 advances versus 2,679 declines**, and small caps lost about **1.01%**.
- **Jio Platforms opened India's largest IPO:** Its roughly **Rs 8,010 crore** anchor book drew **31-times** demand, above **Rs 2.5 lakh crore**, versus the NSE anchor book's **17.8-times** response to **Rs 6,746 crore**. The approximately **Rs 30,000 crore** IPO seeks a valuation near **Rs 10.3 lakh crore** and was already **0.5-times** subscribed within five minutes of opening.
- **The key technical level was 23,000:** Kutumba Rao said sustained trade above **23,000** would keep the market in a safe zone; weakness below it could expose **22,500**. Bank Nifty call interest was concentrated near **56,000**, the put-call ratio was **0.93**, and VIX was around **14.828**.
- **Results were mostly constructive:** HFCL, Piramal Finance, Tatva Chintan, Hindustan Zinc, Varmora Granito, Bajaj Finance, AksharChem and Union Bank were cited positively. Caption Services reported year-on-year revenue growth of **7%**, EBITDA growth of **41%** and PAT growth of **19%**, plus a **5:1** stock split and more than **150,000** app downloads.

## Analysts/segments

- **Kutumba Rao:** Expected consolidation rather than panic, but warned that **$110** crude and a rupee vulnerable to **96-97** without RBI intervention could weigh on the December quarter. He advised staying invested, protecting trading profits with stop-losses and using result season to reshuffle portfolios.
- **Kranthi:** Called Jio Platforms a blue-chip core holding and a stock to buy and hold, while warning that a later block sale by Meta or another pre-IPO investor could signal peak valuation. He estimated their currency-adjusted six-year CAGR at only about **5-6%**, versus roughly **15%** in rupee terms.
- **Ramakrishna:** Preferred evaluating Jio after listing and results. His explicit stock call was to buy/try Aegis Logistics after its pullback, for positional or long-term investors.
