## Global backdrop

- **AI self-regulation:** Trump met technology CEOs, described AI as "super intelligence," and asked the industry to self-regulate rather than announcing government controls; tech-heavy Asian markets reacted positively.
- **Oil supply improving:** Current-month crude fell from about **$106 to $103**, while the next series traded near **$96**. Hormuz shipments were close to pre-war levels and roughly half of Saudi Arabia's damaged East-West Pipeline had been repaired.
- **Bond pressure:** The US ten-year yield was near **5.23%** and the 30-year near **5.56%** after touching **5.61%**, its highest since **2002**. Oil had eased but yields remained elevated.

## Indian markets

- **Historic weak series:** September was among the worst series in **25 years**: Nifty lost roughly **6.7-7%** and Bank Nifty **5.8%**, with both below their **50-day and 200-day** averages.
- **Short positioning:** Market-wide rollovers were **89%**, Nifty rollovers **74%**, and FIIs carried about **2.67 lakh short contracts** into October. Nifty five-year CAGR was only about **5%**, below an FD.
- **Earnings valuation:** Nifty's one-year forward P/E was around **17x**, below its ten-year average. Expected FY26-FY28 profit CAGR was cited at **16% for Nifty 50**, **17% for Midcap 150**, and **27% for Smallcap 250**.
- **FII stress:** Foreign selling approached **Rs 10,000 crore** in a day; DIIs bought around **Rs 7,000 crore**. The rupee crossed **96** intraday and closed near **95.94** after intervention, while September FII G-sec sales were about **Rs 9,200 crore**.
- **Levels/volatility:** Calls were concentrated at **23,000**, puts at **22,500**, and maximum put open interest at **22,700**. PCR improved from about **0.7 to 0.91**; VIX fell from **14.77** to **13.41**.
- **Broader market stronger:** Despite Nifty initially losing about **40 points**, BSE had **1,378 advances / 656 declines** and NSE **1,487 / 546**. KSB Pumps gained **6%**, HFCL hit a **5%** upper circuit, while BSE fell about **3.1%**.
