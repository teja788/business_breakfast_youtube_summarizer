## Global backdrop

- **US technology weakness before Nvidia:** Nasdaq fell **0.74%** and the S&P 500 lost up to **1%**, but Nvidia subsequently reported revenue of **$115.88 billion** versus an expected **$109.15 billion**, EPS of **$2.70**, shareholder returns of **$28.1 billion**, a remaining **$227 billion** repurchase authorization and a **$0.25** quarterly dividend.
- **AI valuation test:** The program said Anthropic would list the next evening at a **$1.33 trillion** valuation, up from **$460 million in September 2021**. Kutumba Rao warned that India would not be immune to a major US AI correction.
- **Mixed macro cues:** Crude was near **$92** and the rupee strengthened to roughly **93.41-93.47** per dollar. Gold weakness was seen as a risk for gold-finance lenders and borrowers.

## Indian markets

- **Large-cap-led advance:** Nifty had gained **96 points**, Sensex **427 points**, and Bank Nifty was leading. Nifty reached a **nine-month high**; **22** constituents were within **5%** of their 52-week highs, while Eternal, Kotak Mahindra Bank and ICICI Bank were already near those highs.
- **Powerful foreign flows:** November FII buying was cited near **Rs 94,963 crore**, or almost **$10 billion**, compared with about **$8.55 billion in December 2020**. The rupee's roughly **75-paise** two-day move was described as its strongest rally in **20 months**.
- **Key levels:** Kutumba Rao and Ramakrishna both identified **25,500** as major Nifty resistance; supports were **25,000** and **24,800**, with the next resistance near **25,800**. Bank Nifty resistance/support levels were cited at **63,635/60,371**, while options suggested **62,300/62,000** for the near term.
- **Breadth stayed weak:** At the open there were **786 advances/969 declines** on NSE and **875/1,153** on BSE. Mid- and small-caps lagged while banks and selected large caps remained stronger.
