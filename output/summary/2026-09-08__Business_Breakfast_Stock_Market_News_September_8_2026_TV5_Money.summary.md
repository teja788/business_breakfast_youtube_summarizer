## Global backdrop
- **Crude and rates remained the main risks:** crude was cited at **$97.22** and moving toward **$100**, while the US ten-year Treasury yield was near **4.8%** with some forecasts of **5%-5.5%**. Qatar warned of an industrial catastrophe if the Strait of Hormuz crisis persisted.

- Nifty had fallen roughly **1,000 points** since August 3, from **24,770 to 23,770**. Supports were placed at **23,735** and **23,600**; Nifty was down about **3.5%** over one month versus Bank Nifty's **1.2%** decline.
