## Global backdrop

- **Crude remained the main constraint:** Brent was near **$114**, while Kutumba Rao cited oil around **$109-110** and doubted a significant Indian rally until it cooled. Trump-Iran negotiations showed little progress despite regional and UN mediation efforts.
- **US technology led overnight:** The S&P 500 gained **0.7%**, Nasdaq **0.8%**, the Dow closed positive and the semiconductor index rose **3%**. Amazon and Intel were due Thursday; Alphabet and Microsoft Wednesday. The Kospi rose **4%**, led by a **7%** rise in SK Hynix.
- **Brazil suffered an election shock:** Lula's unexpected re-election sent the market down as much as **20%** intraday and **14%** at the close, while the currency weakened. India was described as structurally broader and more resilient than Brazil or Korea.
- **The rupee and foreign flows stayed weak:** The rupee closed near **95.15**; FIIs sold about **Rs 1,462 crore** and DIIs bought roughly **Rs 2,600 crore**.

## Indian markets

- **Expiry kept the setup cautious:** Kutumba Rao placed Nifty support near **22,500** and resistance near **22,900**; sustained trade below **22,700** could revisit **22,500**. Bank Nifty support was near **53,500**, with resistance near **54,800**.
- **Volatility remained unfriendly to bulls:** VIX approached **18** intraday and PCR was not supportive. Rollovers and sector-level stock-futures positioning were the key expiry signals.
- **Small caps bounced but quality remained decisive:** The small-cap index rose **1.38%** after many shares had fallen **20-30%**. Breadth was **1,942 advances / 1,449 declines**, but analysts warned against treating a one-day rebound as a return to post-April exuberance.
- **Defense remained a structural theme:** The Defense Acquisition Council was expected to confirm about **Rs 2.12 lakh crore** of orders for helicopters, air defense and drones, with **82%** domestic sourcing.
- **Apollo Micro reported record numbers:** H1 consolidated revenue was **Rs 639 crore**, up **78%**; EBITDA rose **65%** to **Rs 165 crore** and PAT rose **50%** to **Rs 71 crore**. Its current order book was **Rs 1,765 crore**, and management said a Navy ground-mine order could lift it close to **Rs 4,000 crore**, excluding Premier Explosives.
- **Opening trade was negative but stock-specific:** Nifty opened about **70 points** lower, Sensex **270 points** lower and Bank Nifty **80 points** lower. Aequs fell **18%**, while ITC Hotels gained **5%**, Bajel Projects **8%**, Aeroflex nearly **5%**, and Apollo Micro about **4-5%** near **Rs 430**.
