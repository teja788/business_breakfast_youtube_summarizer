## Global backdrop

- **Crude remained near $110 despite heavy traffic through Hormuz:** Physical oil was still around **$70-80**, but shipping and insurance were adding roughly **$30-40 per barrel**. A captain was reportedly receiving a **$100,000** incentive per Strait of Hormuz transit, so prices were unlikely to ease without an Iran-US understanding.
- **US cash markets rose, but morning cues weakened:** Nasdaq gained **1%** and the S&P 500 **0.6%**, with Meta, Tesla and other AI-linked names up **3-5%** after SpaceX's strong result. By morning, Nasdaq futures and Asian screens were negative, KOSPI was down almost **2.2%**, and the US 10-year yield was near **5.09%**.
- **France remained a credit and civil-unrest concern:** The French 10-year yield was described as being at a **22- or 24-year high**, alongside continuing unrest and conflict between local residents and illegal immigrants.
- **Possible positives were approaching:** Kutumba Rao expected possible India-US trade-deal clarity within **one to three weeks**. The rupee reached about **94.47**, appreciating for eight consecutive sessions for the first time in eight years.

## Indian markets

- **The broader correction was severe:** From 52-week highs, the **10-20%** correction bucket contained **42** Midcap 150, **77** Smallcap 250 and **70** Microcap 250 stocks. The **20-30%** bucket contained **35**, **54** and **64**, while the **40-45%** bucket contained **2**, **9** and about **10** respectively.
- **Leverage amplified the sell-off:** The combined NSE-BSE MTF book was about **Rs 1,56,151 crore** on October 19, including about **Rs 1.48 lakh crore** on NSE and **Rs 7,500 crore** on BSE. Forced margin square-offs helped explain why the market fell even as FIIs bought about **Rs 1,614 crore** and DIIs **Rs 1,022 crore**.
- **Jio Platforms pulled liquidity from the secondary market:** Its **Rs 30,000 crore** IPO was fully subscribed in about **100 minutes**, ended day one around **3.16 times** subscribed after a **31-times** anchor response, and had retail demand near **0.73 times**. IPO inflows also helped the rupee reach a four-month high.
- **The weak open confirmed negative breadth:** Nifty fell about **220 points**, Sensex roughly **588-600** and Bank Nifty **345**. A later snapshot showed only **314 advances versus 1,754 declines**, close to **1:6**. International Gemmological Institute gained about **5.5%**, while E-mudhra fell **12.5%** and KEI Industries roughly **7-8%**.

## Analysts/segments

- **Kutumba Rao:** Called the decline a market realignment rather than the end of opportunity. He warned that high retail MTF exposure was a risk even without promoter pledging, but said the correction could become a stock picker's paradise. He expected the difficult phase to last perhaps **two to three months** and preferred patient accumulation of fundamentally sound companies.
- **Kranthi:** Said cooler valuations were creating selective opportunities for long-term investors, though short- and medium-term panic could continue until crude cooled. His explicit viewer call was to hold Shilpa Medicare and add it slowly on dips after its fall from about **Rs 1,100** to near **Rs 830**.
- **Ramakrishna:** Noted that about **325 of the Nifty 500** stocks were below their 200-day averages versus roughly **370** during the war period. A close below **22,600** would require reassessment; intraday, sustained trade below about **22,550** could offer a short, while longs required a hold above **22,697**.
- **Results:** Yash Highvoltage reported revenue growth of **95%**, EBITDA growth of **139%**, PAT growth of **157%**, and margin expansion from **23.3% to 28.1%**. Indian Hotels and Anand Rathi Wealth were cited positively by Ramakrishna.
