## Global backdrop

- **Crude and West Asia remained the main risks:** Brent moved from about **$105** to **$106-107** amid attacks on ships in the Strait of Hormuz and direct Saudi-Houthi missile conflict. The possibility of direct US intervention remained uncertain ahead of the midterm polls.
- **US markets and yields softened:** Micron fell about **3.5%**, while Microsoft closed near a record high. The US 10-year and 30-year yields eased to roughly **5.21%** and **5.56%**. New York Fed commentary suggested no hike would be needed in **2027** after one more possible hike in **2026**.
- **Asian performance diverged:** Korea recovered from the previous day's weakness and Japan traded strongly, but the broader global picture remained negative. The IMF raised India's FY27 growth outlook to **7%**.

## Indian markets

- **Taxation hopes powered a one-day surge:** Nirmala Sitharaman said the government was listening to discussion around STT and equity taxation, but promised no change before the Budget on **February 1**, still **3-3.5 months** away. Nifty nevertheless gained roughly **350 points**.
- **IT led the rally:** The IT index rose **5.22%**, or roughly **1,400 points**. HCL Tech gained about **8.5%**, TCS **7%**, and Tech Mahindra and Infosys around **5%**. Kutumba Rao said IT contributed the last **700-800 points** of the move and warned that other sectors would need to support any profit-booking.
- **Key levels improved but consolidation remained likely:** Nifty crossed **22,500**, **22,600** and ran toward **22,800**. Call writing shifted from **22,800** to **23,000**, with positions also at **23,200**; puts were heavy at **22,500** and building at **22,700**. PCR jumped from **0.73** to **1.16**, while VIX fell from above **15** toward **14**.
- **Domestic money offset foreign selling:** More than **Rs 10 lakh crore** of mutual-fund assets were cited in mid- and small-cap stocks, while foreign investors had sold more than **Rs 3 lakh crore** in the current year, about double the prior year's amount. Nifty still held above **22,000**.
- **Results supported stock-specific activity:** Transformers & Rectifiers' order book rose **32%** to **Rs 7,237 crore**, and Q2 order inflow rose about **160%** to **Rs 3,283 crore**. Tata Elxsi rose almost **7%** at the opening after gaining **5.2%** the prior day. Groww, Canara Bank, MRPL, Tata Technologies, Cyient and Elecon Engineering were among the day's result names.
- **Opening breadth reversed:** BSE initially showed **1,224 advances/923 declines** and NSE **1,361/962**, but NSE later turned negative at **1,159 advances/1,303 declines**. Urban Company's proposed QIP was about **Rs 2,500 crore**.

## Analysts/segments

- **Kutumba Rao:** Expected consolidation after the unexpected rally rather than a runaway move. He highlighted improving option data but warned that a return to **22,700-22,500** could signal the rally was fizzling. He advised stock-specific trading during results season and flagged **5% hydrogen blending** into piped natural gas as a possible new speculative theme.
- **Kranthi:** Treated the finance minister's Bloomberg-event statement as the first positive government signal for equities in **6-8 months**, but said the final STCG/LTCG outcome and Budget action remained unknown. He saw no strong current answer to FPI outflows or medium-term rupee weakness.
- **Ramakrishna:** Said a move above **22,800** was needed to establish higher highs and lows. Nifty RSI improved to **43**, Bank Nifty RSI to **48**, and VIX fell **7.43%** toward **14**. He viewed **22,200** as the apparent floor, suggested an intraday long near **22,600** with a **22,520** stop and **22,700-22,750** targets, and gave no stock pick for the day.
- **IPO focus:** Tonbo Imaging's issue size was cited at **Rs 852 crore**, with multiple marquee pre-IPO investors, while Fusion CX opened with major mutual funds in its anchor book.
