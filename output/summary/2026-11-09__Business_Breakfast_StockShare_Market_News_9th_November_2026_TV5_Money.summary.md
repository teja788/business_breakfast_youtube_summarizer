## Global backdrop

- **AI remains unsettled, not finished:** OpenAI reportedly raised **$11 billion** at a **$1.25 trillion** valuation versus **$850 billion** in March, while Anthropic placed **$3.4 billion** of power-equipment orders for data-center investment. The panel warned against treating the recent AI/data-center selloff as proof that the theme is over.
- **Asian rotation toward India:** Nifty rose **8.4%** in the month while remaining about **4.4%** lower year to date; Kospi fell **11%**, Taiwan **9%**, and Nikkei **6.5%**. Roughly **Rs 50,000 crore** of FPI buying arrived over ten sessions as money rotated out of Korea and Taiwan.
- **Geopolitics and US policy:** A weaker Trump after the midterms and possible softening around Israel/Iran were viewed as favorable for India, though commodity prices and news-driven volatility remain risks.

## Indian markets

- **Consolidation would be healthy:** Kutumba Rao said last week's roughly **6-8.6% as heard** rise was among the strongest in six years. He preferred stabilization after the surge and called this a time for earnings homework, not random stock selection.
- **Large-cap preference:** MSCI India's forward P/E was cited at **18.45** versus **8.84** for Asia excluding Japan. Returning FIIs may favor under-owned defensive large caps, banks and other liquid leaders.
- **Key levels and breadth:** Ramakrishna identified **24,800** as strong Nifty support; VIX rose about **5%** to **18.43**. After the open Nifty traded near **24,917**, down about **70 points**, and NSE breadth was **1,005 advances versus 1,354 declines**.
- **Buy on dips, but use results:** Pharma, banks, metals and cement were stronger areas, while IT and data-center-linked names remained weak. The panel repeatedly emphasized earnings quality and careful position sizing.

## Analysts/segments

- **Indian data-center capacity:** Kutumba Rao said India has about **1.5 GW** of capacity and could reach **10 GW by 2030**, still far below the US. He did not expect US-scale public resistance but said power, water, noise and environmental concerns require serious answers from governments and companies.
- **Corporate commentary:** Azad Engineering had commissioned six dedicated facilities across about **45,000 square meters** for global OEMs. Tonbo Imaging was described as a long-term story after roughly **Rs 24 crore** of profit on **Rs 180 crore** of revenue as heard.
- **Kutumba Rao calls:** He advised **not averaging Techno Electric** before its next result and using a stop, while recommending **Hold** on **Aegis Vopak Terminals** and **Welspun Corp**.
- **Kranthi calls:** He recommended **HDFC Bank Hold**, **Laxmi Organic Avoid** for investment, **Zydus Lifesciences Hold**, **Sai Parenterals Buy with separate capital**, **Shreeji Shipping Global Avoid**, **GNG Electronics Add on dips**, and **Indo Farm Equipment Add slowly** for one to two years.
