## Global backdrop

- **Global cues stabilised:** KOSPI rebounded about **2%** after the prior session's captioned **8% circuit-breaker** move. Nasdaq closed slightly positive, though Nvidia fell **2%** and the semiconductor index about **0.9%**.
- **US yields cooled:** The 10-year yield fell from about **5.37% to 5.07%** in six days, while the 30-year eased from roughly **5.6% to 5.42%**. High US yields and currency depreciation had helped drive about **Rs 90,000 crore** of FII outflows from India during the month.
- **Foreign selling remained heavy:** FIIs sold **Rs 5,285 crore** while DIIs bought **Rs 3,319 crore**, yet expiry effects helped Nifty gain about **1.66%**.

## Indian markets

- **Large caps drove the rally:** IT, banks, Reliance and Bharti Airtel led. Reliance gained roughly **3.4-4%**, or about **Rs 40**, on the Jio Platforms IPO narrative. Mid caps fell about **0.3%** and small caps about **1.3%**, with breadth near **1,200 advances/2,167 declines**.
- **Jio dominated discussion:** The young Ambani generation led the IPO presentation. Pre-IPO Meta-linked shares were cited around **Rs 488** versus an IPO band near **Rs 1,190-1,195**; HFCL promoters' separate early investment was described as growing from about **Rs 47.9 crore to Rs 5,356 crore**, or **11,295%**.
- **Results drove sharp reactions:** Tech Mahindra posted in-line numbers and a **1:1 bonus**. HDFC AMC, HDFC Life, Angel One, L&T Finance, IIFL Finance and Cyient DLM were described positively; Nestle India fell almost **8%**, while BHEL and Canara Bank fell about **6-6.5%** after results.
- **SEBI retained expiry VWAP settlement:** The market welcomed a return to the last-**30-minute** volume-weighted method. Kutumba Rao cited Nifty support at **22,600-22,700** after a move through **23,000**.

## Analysts/segments

- **Kutumba Rao:** Expected consolidation after Nifty's best day in four months, but viewed the high-volume large-cap move as sustainable enough to buy dips through Diwali. Direct taxes rose **13.2%**; six-month STT collections were **Rs 46,840 crore** versus about **Rs 31,000 crore** a year earlier. He reiterated avoiding Yes Bank and preferring SBI.
- **Kranthi:** Explained that private-equity gains reflect early risk and that post-listing returns depend on earnings and valuation. His workshop segment will apply John Templeton's popularity-cycle framework to data centres, hydrogen, nuclear, defence, aerospace and other themes. He gave no clearly attributable stock call.
- **Ramakrishna:** Expected index consolidation. He recommended Karur Vysya Bank only on dips to **Rs 330-335** and put BSE on his buying list after a technical pullback.
