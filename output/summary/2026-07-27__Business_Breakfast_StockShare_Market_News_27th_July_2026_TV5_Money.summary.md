## Global backdrop
- **Gap-up start to a fresh trading week.** GIFT Nifty indicated a positive/gap-up opening of almost **200 points**. The US Ambassador to the UN: **"Trump is giving it a little bit of room."**
- **Crude cooled off hard.** Brent is **down about $10 from the weekend high**, at roughly **$92-93** this morning (Ramakrishna quoted **$93**, "almost 7% down from 100"). The show had flagged that a move near $100 invited a cool-off. Kranthi: the **last three months' average crude price for the OMCs was $104/barrel**; it is now below $100.

- **US/Asia**: Nasdaq fell Friday, **tech selling continued** — **Intel -8% despite beating earnings estimates**; **Alphabet announced good numbers but the stock fell on capex concerns**. **Taiwan down** this morning, other Asian markets bouncing back.
- **Crucial week**: **Fed decision Wednesday night** (India reacts Thursday morning) — a July hike unlikely, but a **September rate hike cannot be ruled out**. **Microsoft, Meta, Amazon and Apple report this week.** **Nifty monthly expiry tomorrow (Tuesday); BSE monthly options expiry Thursday.**
