## Global backdrop
- **GIFT Nifty indicated a gap-up of 250+ points** at one point last night, because **the shadows of war reduced sharply** and **Brent crude fell to ~$82.5**. By this morning crude had jumped back to **$87-88**, so the overnight relief was already fading.
- **West Asia is escalating again**: Iran reportedly fired **multiple ballistic missiles** at Jordan and other targets; the **US military said all were intercepted and all failed**. With no deal, **Trump restarted his threat to demolish Iran's power plants and bridges**. Tensions are not subsiding anywhere.
- **Asia weak**: **Kospi -6%** at the time of the show and **down 40% from its high** (its market cap had crossed **$5 trillion**); **Taiwan and the Nikkei also weak**. The show's point: **lopsided growth** — Korea rode just **SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics**, Taiwan just **TSMC**, and the same stocks are now dragging those indices down. Blame **valuation concerns** and worries about **"super circular financing" in AI**.
- **US last night — the AI trade reversed again.** Traditional IT/services up: **Accenture +6.8%, Cognizant +7%, EPAM +6.6%, Globant +7.5%**. Chips down: **AMD -8%, Intel -6%, SanDisk -14%, Micron -9%, Seagate -8.5%, Western Digital -7%**. **SK Hynix profit +552% but below estimates — ADR -9%.** **Apple has reached $5 trillion** while **Nvidia has slipped from $5.7 trillion to $4.77 trillion**.
