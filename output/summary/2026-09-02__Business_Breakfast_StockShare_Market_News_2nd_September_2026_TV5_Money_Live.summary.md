## Global backdrop
- **War, crude and yields drove the sell-off:** Brent reached **$96** amid renewed US-Iran strikes. The US 10-year yield was **4.80%-4.81%**, Japan around **3.01%**, the UK 30-year **5.15%**, Germany **3.36%** and Australia **5.22%**.
- Saudi Arabia reportedly sold **$3.25 billion** of US bonds. The narrowing US-Japan yield gap, now below **1.5%**, raised the risk of a yen carry-trade unwind.

## Indian markets
- August GST receipts reached **Rs 2 lakh crore**, up **14.8%** year on year, while FCNR inflows crossed **$100 billion**. Net GST growth of **8.3%** was also cited, but manufacturing PMI was described as a five-year low.
- The rupee strengthened below **95** amid RBI intervention. FIIs bought a little over **Rs 1,100 crore** and domestic institutions more than **Rs 1,800 crore**.
- Nifty opened down **235 points**, Sensex more than **700**, and Bank Nifty about **500**. Kutumba Rao flagged **24,200** as resistance, **24,000** as the danger level and **23,500** as downside put support; PCR was **0.89** and VIX **11.19**.
- Ramakrishna saw risk to **23,600**, highest call writing at **24,100** and put writing at **23,500**. He advised avoiding immediate trades and trying a short only on a rebound to **23,940-23,950**.
