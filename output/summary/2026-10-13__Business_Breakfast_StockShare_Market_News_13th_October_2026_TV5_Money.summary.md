## Global backdrop

- **AI trade reversed sharply:** Korea was down almost **7%**, Taiwan was similarly weak, Micron lost **4%** and Intel **3%**. Chinese memory competition from CXMT and bubble warnings from Broadcom CEO Hock Tan were cited, while traditional software names rose **2-3%**.
- **Brent crude reached $106:** Continuing West Asian conflict, Houthi attacks and no visible Russia-Ukraine ceasefire kept energy prices elevated.
- **Popular IPO caution increased:** Zetwerk's proposed **Rs 5,000 crore** issue carried a **Rs 143-151** band. Jio Platforms' reported issue size fell from **Rs 37,000 crore** to **Rs 30,000 crore** and then **Rs 26,000 crore** ahead of the **21st** opening.

## Indian markets

- **Large caps remained weak:** Nifty lost **157 points**, Sensex **542**, while small caps gained **1.42%** and mid caps closed slightly positive. FIIs sold **Rs 1,788 crore** and DIIs bought **Rs 345 crore**.
- **Fund flows explained the divergence:** September inflows were **Rs 9,000 crore** for small caps, **Rs 7,835 crore** for mid caps, **Rs 6,700 crore** for flexi caps, **Rs 4,273 crore** for large-and-mid caps, **Rs 3,850 crore** for multi caps and only **Rs 859 crore** for large caps.
- **Kutumba Rao stayed cautious:** Nifty needed to cross and sustain above **22,500-22,600**. A break lower could expose **21,900** and a further **500-600-point** cut. PCR fell from **1.14** to **0.73**, while VIX rose more than **6%** to about **15.25**.
- **Ramakrishna saw consolidation risk:** A lower break could revisit **21,700** and possibly the 2024 election low near **21,300**. Nifty RSI was near **34**, with another **1-2 weeks** of consolidation possible.
- **Results were mixed with positive details:** RBL Bank's PAT rose to **Rs 498 crore** from **Rs 192 crore**, versus **Rs 234 crore** in Q1, while NII gained **37%**. HCL Tech raised the lower end of guidance from **1-4%** to **2.5-4%** despite a modest revenue miss.
- **Opening breadth was positive but uneven:** Nifty opened down **40 points**, Sensex up **110**, and Bank Nifty down roughly **130-160**. NSE breadth was **1,080 advances/637 declines**, while mid caps fell **0.32%**.
