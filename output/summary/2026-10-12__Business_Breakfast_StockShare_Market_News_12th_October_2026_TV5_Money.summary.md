## Global backdrop

- **Dollar and energy pressure remained central:** The dollar index was cited near **102.5**, while India imports about **5 million barrels of oil per day**. The RBI was said to be supplying dollars to oil-marketing companies through a special window and tightening forex-derivative rules to curb spot-market and speculative demand.
- **Crude held near $103 despite geopolitical shocks:** The programme cited continuing supply, G7 reserve releases and higher OPEC production as stabilisers, even as the US planned to buy **40 lakh tonnes** of Russian diesel and Yemen-based Houthis reportedly targeted Riyadh International Airport.
- **Asian markets sharply outperformed India:** A cited comparison turned **Rs 100** invested from October 2024 into **Rs 255** in Korea and **Rs 217** in Taiwan, versus **Rs 90** in Nifty, or a **minus 10%** return. Stronger overseas earnings growth was presented as the main explanation.
- **Input costs clouded earnings:** Kranthi expected a modest quarter with relatively few negative surprises but no major positive surprises, as companies either absorbed higher raw-material costs or passed them to consumers.

## Indian markets

- **An eight-week losing streak paused:** Nifty and Sensex ended the prior week marginally positive after Friday's rally, while mid- and small-cap indices got relief after **4 weeks** of declines. FII selling still exceeded **Rs 30,000 crore**, broadly offset by domestic institutions.
- **Currency and inflation risks persisted:** The rupee closed near **96.73**, and Kutumba Rao warned that elevated crude, commodities, inflation and tight liquidity could weigh on rate- and credit-sensitive sectors.
- **Key technical levels remained tight:** Kutumba Rao placed resistance at **22,600** and **22,800**, with **23,000** possible only after a sustained, volume-backed breakout. Support was **22,400**. He cited implied volatility near **14.53/15.77** and VIX above **15** as negative for bulls.
- **Ramakrishna watched a broader range:** A move over **22,800** and the 200-SMA could restore strength, while a break below **22,200** could extend the decline. Bank Nifty had risen **741 points**, or **1.36%**, and PCR reached **1.14**, while VIX gained **5.89%** to **14.37**.
- **The opening was broadly positive:** Nifty opened up **123 points**, Sensex **362** and Bank Nifty about **50**. BSE breadth was **1,735 advances/629 declines**, and NSE breadth **1,852/450**. Poonawalla Fincorp gained roughly **6-8%** after results.
- **IPOs and results dominated the week:** Eight IPOs were discussed, including defence-linked Tonbo Imaging. The programme also referenced a coming Jio Platforms IPO and expected quarterly results to dictate sector and stock performance.

## Analysts/segments

- **Kutumba Rao:** Expected selective stability if **22,400** held, advised caution in sectors exposed to higher fuel/input costs, and said results should guide sector selection. His clearly attributable stock guidance was to accumulate Avanti Feeds slowly and hold ITC, Jio Financial and CDSL with patience.
- **Kranthi:** Expected a modest earnings season and said CG Power and TD Power were both excellent but different businesses. He advised holding CG Power rather than booking a loss and taking any TD Power exposure as a fresh position.
- **Ramakrishna:** Suggested a Nifty long only above **22,670-22,675** after a **5-minute** hold, with **22,600** as stop-loss. He also recommended fresh buying in PVR Inox with a **Rs 1,225** stop-loss.
- **SCTL clarification:** Vasanth said SCTL's **51%** G-Scale stake and roughly **Rs 500 crore** investment remained on schedule. Management retained guidance for about **90%** revenue and profit growth, **Rs 160 crore** profit and **Rs 250 crore** data-centre revenue; the cited **486 MW** referred to promoter experience at CtrlS, not an SCTL order.
