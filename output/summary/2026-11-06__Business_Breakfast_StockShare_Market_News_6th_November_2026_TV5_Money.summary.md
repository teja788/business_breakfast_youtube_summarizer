## Global backdrop

- **AI correction intensified:** Nasdaq entered a technical correction, down about **10%** from its high; the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell **12%**. Roughly **$2.3 trillion** of US market value was said to have vanished in one session, including about **$1 trillion** across the Magnificent Seven.
- **Chip and mega-cap damage:** Nvidia reportedly lost about **$440 billion** of market capitalization and Jensen Huang about **$15 billion** of personal wealth. In the S&P 500, **174 stocks** entered bear-market territory; in Nasdaq 100, **59** did so.
- **Political uncertainty:** Democratic control of both chambers while Trump remains president raised fears of policy paralysis, especially around data-center buildout. Korea, Taiwan and Japan also fell roughly **4-6%**, with Kospi trading halted for **20 minutes**.
- **Kutumba Rao's contrarian view:** He did not expect a sustained AI crash and viewed lower US AI-stock prices as an opportunity, while warning that India may be over-reading a short burst of FII buying.

## Indian markets

- **India as an AI hedge:** FIIs bought about **Rs 15,000 crore** in cash in the prior session and roughly **Rs 25,000 crore** including futures. Seven-session buying was near **Rs 40,000 crore**, and about **one lakh option contracts** saw short covering.
- **Large caps should dominate:** The panel expected India to fall less and rise more than global peers, but emphasized that foreign flows favor large caps. Mid- and small-caps may underperform selectively.
- **Technical caution:** Nifty futures around **24,500-24,600** were viewed as a buy-on-dips zone; **24,000** needed to hold after the rapid six-session rally. PCR rose to **1.29**, while VIX remained elevated at **15.58** and needed to fall below **14** for a more durable bull advantage.
- **Breadth weakened after the open:** Market breadth was **536 advances versus 1,293 declines**. IT led and the IT index rose **1.32%**, but data-center counters such as Netweb, E2E Networks and SCTL fell roughly **5-6%**.

## Analysts/segments

- **SCTL interview:** Q2 revenue rose **62%**, EBITDA about **79-80%**, and PAT **38%** year on year; first-half revenue rose **53%**, EBITDA about **56.5%**, and PAT **32.2%**. The company maintained about **Rs 1,450 crore** FY2027 guidance, including **Rs 250 crore** from AI data centers.
- **SCTL investments:** The company committed **Rs 487 crore** to G-Scale, invested **Rs 125 crore** for about **33.55%**, and awaited approval for another **17.5%** to take ownership near **51%**. About **Rs 71 crore** went into GL Haco as heard. G-Scale operations slipped **15-20 days** into December.
- **Kutumba Rao on governance:** The Tukkuguda lease costs **Rs 50 lakh monthly**, or about **Rs 6 crore annually**. He advised SCTL to simplify its eight-subsidiary structure by merging wholly owned entities such as S2, but this was governance advice rather than a stock call.
- **Stock guidance:** Kutumba Rao explicitly said better prices should come in **Amara Raja** and **JBM Auto** and advised not labeling them bad stocks. Kranthi discussed seasonality and strong underlying businesses but did not issue a clear stock action.
