## Global backdrop

- **Risk-on session:** Nasdaq gained almost **1.7%**, Dow and S&P about **1.1%**, with Nvidia up **2.5%**, AMD **6.5%**, and Intel **7.7%**. Japan rose about **0.8%** despite a **25-basis-point** hike.
- **India growth upgrade:** Moody's raised India's FY27 GDP forecast from **6% to 7%**.
- **Cooling pressure:** The US 30-year yield eased from about **5.4% to 5.27%**, the 10-year from **4.99% to 4.93%**, and Brent to roughly **$103-104**. A move into **$90-95** would materially benefit India.

## Indian markets

- **Broader rebound:** Nifty gained **0.2%**, midcap **0.8%**, and smallcap **0.9%**. VIX fell to **12.29**, PCR reached **1**, and **23,000** was treated as the base, with resistance at **23,500** and a possible **23,800** attempt.
- **Foreign versus domestic flows:** FIIs sold about **Rs 3,300 crore** and domestic institutions bought about **Rs 3,600 crore**; the rupee closed around **95.94** after moving beyond 96 intraday.
- **IPO demand:** Karamtara Engineering listed about **40%** higher, Rentomojo **32%**, Steamhouse India **26%**, and LCC Projects **18%**. NSE was about **43% subscribed** after day one and its grey-market premium recovered toward **Rs 200**.
- **Apollo Micro expansion:** Management targeted **40-50%** current-year growth, more than **100%** next year, and group revenue above **Rs 3,000 crore in FY28**, against roughly **Rs 900 crore** last year. A **55,000 sq ft** facility is expanding to about **6 lakh sq ft**.

## Analysts/segments

- **Kutumba Rao:** Expected a possible close near **23,500** if weekend selling was absorbed, saw Tata Sons as an overhang, and favored selective earnings-visible names. Explicit calls were to hold Jai Balaji until results and then sell on a rally, and to hold Pennar Industries for a possible **10-20%** rebound toward **Rs 210-215**.
- **Kranthi:** Characterized the market as buy-on-dips/sell-on-rallies while crude remains the key variable. He saw sentiment shifting from negative to neutral above **23,000**, with profit-taking appropriate near **24,000**, but gave no clearly attributable stock-specific call.
- **Ramakrishna:** Saw mean reversion from oversold conditions. A five-minute hold above **23,360-23,363** could permit a long; for Laurus Labs, start with **20-25%** of the intended position and add on a **10%** dip.
