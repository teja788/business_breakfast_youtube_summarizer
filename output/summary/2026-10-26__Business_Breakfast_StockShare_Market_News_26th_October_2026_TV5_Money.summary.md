## Global backdrop

- **Risk cues remained weak:** The Dow fell about **1.25%**, while the S&P and Nasdaq also closed negative. Asian markets were weak, though US futures were marginally positive.
- **Crude was India's main macro overhang:** Kutumba Rao cited crude near **$110** and Kranthi said it had touched about **$116** before easing toward **$114**. A sustained move toward **$100 per barrel** was seen as necessary for a confirmed medium-term rally.
- **Currency and political risks persisted:** The rupee closed near **94.93**. Kutumba Rao highlighted Lula's Brazilian election victory and the approaching US mid-term elections as policy-sensitive global events.
- **Precious metals had weakened:** Gold was testing roughly **$4,000 per ounce** at a three-month or multi-month low, while silver risked falling below **Rs 2 lakh**. Kutumba Rao preferred waiting for clarity after the US mid-terms, with a speculative gold buy only near **$4,000** and a strict **$3,900** stop-loss.

## Indian markets

- **Friday extended the decline:** Nifty opened higher, reached **22,787**, fell to **22,372** and closed about **87 points** lower near **22,600**. Bank Nifty opened at **54,965**, ranged between **54,990** and **53,942**, and closed more than **600 points** lower at **54,287**. Sensex ranged from **73,766** to **72,452** and closed **125 points** down at **73,123**, about **650 points** above its low.
- **Leverage drove broader-market stress:** MTF positions were said to have expanded from about **Rs 35,000 crore** to nearly **Rs 1,35,000 crore** over the past year. Forced unwinding in leveraged mid- and small-cap positions was the main explanation for repeated intraday selling.
- **Jio liquidity was temporarily locked:** The roughly **Rs 30,000 crore** Jio IPO was subscribed around **22 times** and carried a roughly **20%** grey-market premium. Both analysts expected some relief when refunds returned money to the secondary market.
- **Nifty's key pivot was 22,500:** Calls and puts were concentrated there, with calls also heavy at **23,000**. Kutumba Rao warned against fresh buying while Nifty remained below **22,500**, identified support near **22,340-22,350**, and suggested a **22,200** stop-loss for any attempted support-zone trade.
- **Volatility stayed hostile to bulls:** VIX had risen to **16.86**, PCR was below roughly **0.88**, Bank Nifty puts were concentrated at **53,000** and calls at **55,000**. A dead-cat bounce was possible, but the broader trend remained weak and stock-specific.

## Analysts/segments

- **Kutumba Rao:** Recommended adequate large-cap allocation, avoiding overleveraged positions, overweighting IT selectively, watching pharma for a rebound, and reacting carefully to results. He said good results would be rewarded and bad results punished.
- **Kranthi:** Urged long-term investors to deploy only **10-20%** of available cash gradually around current levels, with at least a one-year horizon, and to continue SIPs through the downtrend. IT valuations were becoming attractive, while AI revenue at Infosys was said to have reached roughly **10-15%**.
- **Results and opening action:** Tata Consumer, Paytm, ITC Hotels, Bank of Maharashtra, Mankind, Sona BLW and Nazara were among results due. At the open, Nifty traded near **22,487**, Sensex near **72,820** and Bank Nifty near **54,160**; Nifty then slipped to about **22,450**, down around **160 points**.
- **Volume leaders:** Meghmani Organics rose more than **7%**, B L Kashyap about **9.1%**, and Automotive Stampings & Assemblies **11%**. Infosys was down almost **2%**, while large-cap leadership remained limited.
