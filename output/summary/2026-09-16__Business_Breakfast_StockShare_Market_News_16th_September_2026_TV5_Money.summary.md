## Global backdrop

- **Fed decision:** Markets assigned a **94%** probability to a **25-basis-point/0.25%** US rate increase, the first since **July 2023**. The hike itself was priced in; Kevin Warsh's guidance on further increases was the main risk.
- **Oil disruption:** Brent traded above **$108** after Houthi damage to Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline pushed September European deliveries to **November**, while Libyan production stopped amid protests.
- **Bond/currency pressure:** The US 10-year yield was **4.983%**, just below **5%**. FIIs withdrew more than **Rs 5,000 crore** from Indian bonds in three days; the rupee weakened to about **95.96** per dollar.
- **War cost:** A US congressional study put five months of the US-Iran war at **$38 billion** and another month's cost at **$2-3 billion**, with core CPI potentially rising **0.3%** and overall inflation **0.5%**.

## Indian markets

- **UPI MDR:** Merchant UPI transactions above **Rs 2,000** will carry a **0.4%** charge capped at **Rs 300**; some capital-market transactions may carry **0.02%**. Estimated system revenue is **Rs 22,000 crore**, benefiting payment platforms and banks such as Yes Bank.
- **Critical Nifty levels:** Nifty lost more than **1,000 points** from **24,800** to around **23,100**. Kutumba Rao identified **23,000** and the June-July low near **23,070** as crucial; calls were concentrated at **23,400**, puts at **22,800** and even **22,500**, and VIX rose to **13.43**.
- **Opening recovery:** After a roughly **470-480 point** high-to-low fall the prior day, Nifty opened up **92-150 points**. Paytm rose **6-7%**, MobiKwik about **5%**, and Yes Bank **4%** on the MDR news.
- **Themes to prepare:** Data centers, power, pharma/health care, chemicals, midcap IT and selected jewellery companies were highlighted. A stronger dollar was negative for base metals and gold financiers.
