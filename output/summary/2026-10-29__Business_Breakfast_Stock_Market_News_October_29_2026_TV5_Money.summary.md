## Global backdrop

- **The Fed was a non-event:** US rates were unchanged despite inflation cited at **3.7%**. Kutumba Rao suggested the midterm-election backdrop may have discouraged another increase and said a modest December move remained possible.
- **Crude and the rupee remained the main risks:** Oil stayed sticky near **$114-115**, while the rupee slipped to **95.28 per dollar** even as FIIs bought about **Rs 900 crore** for a second session; DIIs bought roughly **Rs 643 crore**.
- **US earnings produced sharp stock reactions:** Microsoft rose **3%** after strong numbers; Google fell **10%**, ServiceNow **17%**, and Oracle rose **11%** after guiding toward **$235 billion** of revenue by 2030. SK Hynix warned that memory-chip shortages could persist through **2030**.
- **Israel remained uncertain:** Netanyahu was expected to form a government after no party secured a clear majority, with possible implications for the West Asian crisis.

## Indian markets

- **Jio Platforms dominated the session:** The listing lifted Reliance group market capitalization to about **Rs 30.26 lakh crore**, ahead of Tata's **Rs 25.39 lakh crore**. Jio was described as the world's fifth-largest telecom company at about **$137 billion**.
- **Reliance's valuation gap was highlighted:** Reliance's Jio holding was placed near **Rs 8.77 lakh crore**; a sum-of-the-parts estimate of **Rs 21.2 lakh crore** compared with Reliance Industries' roughly **Rs 19.77 lakh crore** market cap, implying about a **20% discount**.
- **Expiry kept the index setup cautious:** Kutumba Rao watched **22,700**, the actual close near **22,698**, put support at **22,500**, call concentration at **23,000**, PCR at **1.07**, and VIX at **16.24**. Bank Nifty option activity centered on **55,000**, with call writing near **54,000**.
- **The bias was buy-on-dips, not shorting:** Kutumba Rao expected mild small- and mid-cap recovery after last week's correction, while still warning that crude and volatility kept the market in a risky zone.
- **Results were mixed:** Good or better numbers were cited from Adani Ports, JSW Energy, PNB Housing, TVS Motor, City Union Bank, Vedanta, Craftsman Automation and KPR Mill; Pidilite, United Spirits, Force Motors, Bandhan Bank and several smaller companies disappointed.
- **Opening trade was positive:** Nifty gained about **35 points**, Sensex **155**, and Bank Nifty **66**. Sanofi Consumer rose **12%**, Jupiter Wagons **6%**, United Spirits **4%**, and MosChip fell **7%** after poor results despite a preferential issue at **Rs 222**.
