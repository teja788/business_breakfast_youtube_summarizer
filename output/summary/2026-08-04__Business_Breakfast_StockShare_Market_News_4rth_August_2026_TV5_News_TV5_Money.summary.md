## Indian markets -- Closing Auction Session (CAS) chaos
- **The dominant story of the day**: the **first-ever Closing Auction Session (CAS)** on the NSE on the previous trading day created **massive confusion**. F&O trading was halted at 3:15 PM, and the auction window (3:20-3:28) for 219 F&O stocks produced a **200-point pricing mismatch** between Nifty spot (official close 24,774) and Nifty futures (close 24,573). The true "correct" close was around **24,554**.
- The mismatch extended to individual counters -- **ICICI Bank futures closed at 1,447 vs. spot at 1,460**, and similar gaps appeared in HDFC Bank, Reliance, Infosys.
- **Only ~50,000 unique PANs** and **~550 brokers** participated in the CAS; the thin participation let a few strong hands distort prices.
- **NSE CAS volume**: Rs 1,200 crores (NSE) vs. Rs 10 crores (BSE) -- negligible.
- All three analysts and the anchor were highly critical of the CAS rollout. **Kutumba Rao called it "a big faux pas"** by regulators -- "the fox got burnt imitating the tiger." He noted that even **Nasdaq has no CAS** despite being the most important exchange. **Ramakrishna** noted that the lack of a cancellation mechanism for limit orders (unlike the pre-open session) caused price distortion. **Kranthi** said it was introduced in line with global standards but needs more trading sessions to stabilise.
- **Today is Tuesday options expiry** plus a **settlement day** -- additional CAS confusion could cause settlement headaches.

## Indian markets -- actual positioning
- **Stripping out the CAS distortion**, the Nifty's "real" close of **~24,554 represents a good breakout** above the 24,400-500 level.
- **GIFT Nifty indicated a flat opening**; actual opening showed **Nifty -170 pts, Sensex +130 pts, Bank Nifty -440 pts** -- a rare divergence caused by the CAS pricing basis.
- **Mid-cap and small-cap indices closed at all-time highs** on the previous day; Bank Nifty, Nifty, Sensex all closed strongly.
- **FIIs continued buying**: Rs 922 crores (previous day), continuing a July streak of Rs 20,000 crores bought; August buying continues. FIIs also **covered ~25% of their short positions** (from 2.06 lakh contracts to ~1.56 lakh). **DIIs bought Rs 1,571 crores**.
