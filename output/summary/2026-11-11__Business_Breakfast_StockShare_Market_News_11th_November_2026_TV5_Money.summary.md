## Global backdrop

- **Crude supports India:** Crude fell from roughly **$115-$118** days earlier and **$100** Friday to around **$92**, briefly reaching **$89**. The panel viewed this, easing geopolitical risk and rotation away from US AI exposure as supportive for India.
- **US inflation and yields:** October CPI was cited at **0.4% month on month** and core CPI at **0.3%**, about **0.1 percentage point** above expectations. A possible December rate rise and a US 10-year yield back above **5%** were risks.
- **AI correction, not collapse:** Kutumba Rao said AI demand is becoming utility-like and will not disappear; returns will depend on earnings and capex discipline. Anthropic's reported IPO and the controversy around Nvidia management were presented as major sentiment tests.

## Indian markets

- **Banks and large caps lead:** Monday's Nifty gain was only **55-60 points**, while Bank Nifty rose **890 points** to **59,741**, about **2,000 points** below its **61,764** high. FIIs bought **Rs 7,212 crore**, DIIs sold **Rs 7,172 crore**, and FII net shorts fell to an 11-month low of **91,000 contracts**.
- **Consolidation expected:** Kutumba Rao did not expect a runaway rally. He said DII cash redeployment could reveal new sectors, while unemployment at a six-quarter high remained a macro caution.
- **Key technical levels:** Nifty support was placed at **25,000** and **24,800**, with call resistance at **25,500**. Bank Nifty support was **59,000**, followed by resistance at **60,500** and **61,000-61,200**. Nifty RSI was cited at **77.88** and VIX above **17**.
- **Strong results and opening:** Forty-seven Nifty 50 companies reportedly delivered average Q2 revenue growth of **21.28%**, gross-profit growth of **19.88%**, and net-profit growth of about **21.22%**. Nifty opened near **25,340**, up roughly **270-280 points**, with strong breadth.

## Analysts/segments

- **Kutumba Rao calls:** Continue SIPs in **NSE, Manipal Hospitals, Hyundai Motor India and Sanofi India**, but shift away from **Bajaj Housing Finance** toward another NBFC or bank. He called **JP Power** a lottery-ticket stock and not suitable to hold without high risk, and limited **Restaurant Brands Asia** to a positional trade.
- **Kranthi calls:** **Add Ather Energy on dips** for two years; **watch India Glycols** until results on the **13th** and the earnings call on the **18th**; and use rallies to **exit RIR Power**, which she said she had never recommended.
- **Result watch:** Tata Power, Bajaj Hindusthan, Hindustan Copper, Lalithaa Jewellery, Power Mech and Mishra Dhatu Nigam were highlighted. Avanti Feeds profit fell **55%**; Olectra reported revenue of **Rs 687 crore** and profit rising from **Rs 49 crore to Rs 52 crore**.
