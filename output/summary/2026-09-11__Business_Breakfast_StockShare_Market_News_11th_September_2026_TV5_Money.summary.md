## Global backdrop

- **Crude shock:** Brent touched about **$110**, settled near **$108** after a **7.5%** overnight rise, and NYMEX was **$102.73**. Houthi control of a Yemeni Red Sea port and an attack on Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline threatened two major oil routes.
- **Rates and bonds:** US 10-year yields were roughly **4.59%-4.967%** and 30-year yields **5.37%**, a **19-year high**. Trump said the war and high oil prices could persist until the November midterms, about **eight weeks/two months** away.
- **Macro risks:** Kutumba Rao highlighted the proposed **$5,000** Trump dividend, potentially costing **$1-1.2 trillion** against nearly **$40 trillion** in US debt, and warned that crude around **$107** could force Indian petrol/diesel hikes and raise inflation.

## Indian markets

- **NSE IPO:** The offer size was cut from **Rs 30,000 crore** to **Rs 22,562 crore**, with a **Rs 1,700-1,785** band and implied valuation near **Rs 4.42 lakh crore**. NSE traded near **Rs 1,985** unlisted with about **Rs 200** GMP; issue opening was scheduled for **September 17**.
- **Lower range:** Kutumba Rao said the Nifty's former **24,000-24,500** band had shifted to **23,000-23,500**. Put interest was concentrated at **23,000**, calls at **23,500**, and heavy call writing at **23,400**; PCR was about **0.77** and VIX below **12**.
- **Weak opening:** Nifty opened down **235 points** near **23,240**, Sensex **700**, and Bank Nifty **650**. BSE breadth was **555 advances vs 1,578 declines**. Ramakrishna identified resistance at **23,600** and support around **23,050-23,100**, with **23,200** as an intraday-long stop.
- **Changing leadership:** IPOs, new listings, defence, aerospace, engineering, data centers, pharma, health care and selected power names were favored over older large-cap, banking, cement, railway and renewable themes. Mutual-fund inflows into mid/small caps reached a **four-month high**.

- **Kutumba Rao:** Called IPO booms cyclical, warned against valuation-driven euphoria, and advised range-bound caution rather than excessive bearishness. He explicitly said hold TurtleMint near **Rs 131**, and exit SpiceJet and Lakshmi Organics on rallies.
- **Kranthi:** Warned that lower derivatives volumes and possible weekly-expiry changes could weigh on NSE in the short/medium term. He saw Leap India potentially falling from **Rs 140** toward **Rs 110-120**, preferred buying OFIL after its correction, advised waiting on Premier Energies, and said hold the RVNL/IRFC/IREDA/Ola Electric/IOB basket through consolidation.
- **Ramakrishna:** Warned of crude-, rupee- and expiry-driven volatility, preferred avoiding trades before the long weekend, and allowed only a compulsory long with **23,200** as stop-loss.
