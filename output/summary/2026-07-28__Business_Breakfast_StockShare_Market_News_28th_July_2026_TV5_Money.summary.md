## Global backdrop
- **Asia is bleeding on a chip crash.** The **Kospi fell 8%**, trading was **halted for 20 minutes**, and it is **down almost 8.5%** — the index is effectively two stocks, **Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix**, both falling. **Nikkei -3 to -4%**, **Taiwan -4%**.
- **US overnight**: **Nvidia -4 to 5%**, **Western Digital -4%**, **Seagate -4%**, **SK Hynix ADR -7.5%** (now **below its $149 ADR issue/listing price**), **AMD -5%**, **SanDisk -11%**, **Micron -2%**. **ASML -8.5%**. After a **two-to-three-year monster rally**, chip stocks are falling.
- **Two reasons cited**: (1) **China competition** — Chinese firms are already making **DUV (deep ultraviolet) lithography machines** domestically, threatening **ASML's near-monopoly** and, with it, Western dominance; (2) renewed anxiety over **circular AI financing** (Nvidia invests in OpenAI, OpenAI orders from Nvidia; Nvidia into Anthropic; Apple into Nvidia, then orders from Nvidia).
- **Nvidia's own announcements unsettled the market**: **$750 billion of deals** and a **$250 billion, 10-gigawatt Ohio data centre** investment with various partners were read **negatively**. Nvidia also launched an **Open Source AI Alliance** with ~**40 organisations** (Microsoft, SpaceX [as heard], Palantir [as heard], IBM); **Anthropic did not join** — the show recalled Anthropic's letter arguing the global AI build-out should slow down.
- **The anti-AI trade**: money rotated into software. In the US, **Accenture, Globant, EPAM +3-5%**; the **Infosys ADR +5%**.
- **Crude is the big relief**: Brent near **$87.22**, with **October and November contracts near or below $85** — a macro positive. On Friday crude had been **above $100**.
- **CXMT** (anchor's note) did **Asia's biggest IPO** — listed Monday, instantly the **most valuable company on the Chinese market** at roughly a **$488 billion market cap** (almost half a trillion), on an **$8.6 billion (~Rs 70,000 crore) issue** — cited as evidence of China's strength, versus the Jio and NSE IPOs being discussed here.

## Analysts / segments

### Kutumba Rao (present — main market view plus most of the Q&A)
- **Neither favourable nor unfavourable — "status quo," stabilising.** Factors are changing: crude down, and the **rupee's biggest two-month gain, closing at 95.89**, indirectly reflected well on equities. With this status quo, the **RBI will hold rates at the next meeting** — neither cut nor hike — and **the commentary will be the key**. The **US Fed decision** matters most, since **FII flows are by and large dictated by the Fed**.
- **The chip crash is no surprise.** He had said last week that after the chip run-up this becomes a **sell-on-rallies trade, not a buy-on-dips rally**: **SanDisk peaked above 2,000 and is now closer to 1,200** — worth watching whether it enters bear-market territory. But Korean/Taiwanese/US chip profit-booking **will not rotate into India unless the rupee strengthens**, which is why he is watching **FCNR(B) flows** closely: initial numbers **not too bullish but positive** and comparable to the **2013 issue**, with **$60-70 billion** possible; success would **settle the rupee** and be **slightly positive for markets**.
- **IT is the emerging defensive bet.** A **buy-on-dips has been running in the underweight IT sector for a month**, with value buying on every correction, and broking houses are moving IT from **underweight to neutral or positive**.
- **Banks**: **FII exposure is being raised in PSU banks and cut in private banks**; PSU bank **NPAs and NII are decent**. **Bank of Baroda** — even with its **biggest provision made in the UAE**, adding that back it is an **excellent number**; a **cyber attack** may make it weak today, but **at lower levels it can become a good bet**.
- **Levels and positioning**: in the **August** monthly options, **highest OI at 24,200**, with OI also building at **24,500**; **puts developing at 23,800**, making **24,000 and 23,800 the major downside supports**. The question is whether **24,000 can be crossed** on short covering or whether the market **fizzles out flat** after yesterday's rally — but **defending 23,800 has become very important**; **23,600 was almost breached the week before last and recovered from there**, so **that band is very crucial**. **PCR jumped from 0.85 to 1.11**; **VIX well below 13 at 12.66** after a **~10% single-day fall** — **below 12 would be a big positive**.

### Kranthi (present — thematic view plus one stock call)
- **Defence / aerospace / space is peaking into a boom.** **Skyroot's private satellite launch — almost the first in Asia — is a proud moment for the country and for Hyderabad**, and **allied and accessory industries are developing as a cluster** there. But **investors must be careful: companies that know nothing about defence are changing their names to add "defence."** He compared it to the **1990s TMT (technology, media, telecom) bubble** — the same frenzy now runs across the **three domains of defence, aerospace and space**, and it is **becoming quite peak**.
- **Automobile and ancillary companies are entering the space** as domestic production has to rise, and — endorsing **Kutumba Rao's** earlier point — **engineering precision companies** will see activity because they already have some **skill**, and **technology is not easy**.

He said he told his own **PTC** [as heard] management to look at Hyderabad; **Lucknow** has built a decent cluster in five years, but **Hyderabad and Bengaluru are the key places**.

Hold for now and the price will come back, but **it is a fully priced-in stock** — such business models carry a **45 PE** — so **book profit when rallies come**.
