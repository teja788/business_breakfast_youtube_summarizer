## Global backdrop

- **AI stocks sold off sharply:** Nasdaq fell about **2.6%**, Japan's Nikkei **1.72%**, South Korea's KOSPI **2%**, and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index roughly **6.7-7%**. Amazon lost **4.6%**, Nvidia **4.4%**, AMD **8.1%**, Sandisk about **8.5%** and Seagate roughly **8%**; about **$800 billion** of market value was wiped from Meta, Amazon, Microsoft and Nvidia in one day.
- **Traditional IT benefited from the AI reversal:** Infosys ADR gained **4%** ahead of results. Since October 12, Nifty IT had risen **13%**; Tech Mahindra was up about **24%**, TCS and Wipro **18%**, HCL Tech **16%**, and Infosys **15%**.
- **Bond yields provided one positive cue:** The US 10-year yield cooled from the previously discussed **5.1-5.3%** range to about **4.96%** as investors moved toward bonds. Lower US yields were seen as favourable for Indian banks and financial services.
- **Crude and geopolitics remained major risks:** Brent was near **$114**, with effective import cost including shipping and insurance estimated at **$150-160**. A report suggested possible new US-Israel attacks on Iran after the November 3 US mid-term elections.

## Indian markets

- **Jio Platforms dominated primary-market liquidity:** By day two, the IPO was subscribed **5.6 times** overall, with retail at **1.54 times**, HNIs **5.97 times** and QIBs **13.29 times**. It had attracted roughly **Rs 3.77 lakh crore**, while its grey-market premium was around **Rs 240**, or **21%**.
- **Four IPO listings were due:** Bonfiglioli Transmissions had been subscribed **46 times**, Tonbo Imaging **133 times**, Sahajanand Medical Technologies **14 times**, and the Shell India SME issue **58 times**.
- **Leverage and breadth were still weak:** Kutumba Rao warned that stocks with **6-8%** MTF exposure had fallen **15-25%** as brokers cut leveraged positions. Maximum put open interest was at **22,500**, **22,600** was also important, VIX had jumped to **15.76**, and the put-call ratio was near **0.85**.
- **The positive headline indices masked distribution:** Pre-open indications were Nifty **+80**, Sensex **+544** and Bank Nifty about **+100**. After opening, Nifty was up **93** and Sensex **490**, while Bank Nifty was down **0.16%**. Breadth was only **611 advances versus 1,370 declines**, and IT led with a gain of almost **3%**.
- **Institutional buying did not prevent weakness:** FIIs bought about **Rs 3,500 crore** and domestic institutions about **Rs 3,000 crore**, yet the prior session was negative. The rupee weakened about **45 paise** to **94.82**, with its behaviour increasingly linked to crude.

## Analysts/segments

- **Kutumba Rao:** Expected consolidation after three falling sessions and advised caution in leveraged small and mid caps. A breach of **22,500** would add downside risk, while a PCR closer to **0.7** could create a contrarian positional-buying opportunity. He also warned about banking cyber-security risks and possible macro pressure in November-December.
- **Ramakrishna:** Saw signs that the fall could be arrested after doji and high-wave candles, with RSI near **44**, PCR improving from **0.78 to 0.88**, and VIX at **15.76**. Intraday, he suggested a short below **22,700** or a long only after a **5-10 minute** hold above **22,785**.
- **Results:** HPCL's gross refining margin reached **$32.2**, versus the historically strong **$15-16** range. Infosys, JSW Steel, Dr Reddy's, BPCL, CG Power, Torrent Pharma, IndusInd Bank, SBI Cards and SBI Funds Management were among the important results due.
- **Kranthi:** Was traveling by air to Tirupati and did not participate, so no Kranthi stock call was attributable.
