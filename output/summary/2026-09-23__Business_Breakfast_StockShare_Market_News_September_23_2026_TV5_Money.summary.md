## Global backdrop

- **Crude below $100:** Brent traded around **$98-99** as Saudi Arabia moved toward restarting its East-West pipeline and oil continued moving through the Strait of Hormuz. Trump said US-Iran contacts were positive but deferred any peace deal until after midterms in roughly **six weeks**.
- **US technology:** Nasdaq rose **0.45%** and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index **2%**. Apple touched about **$5 trillion** market value, AMD was described as entering the **$1 trillion** club, and agentic-AI interest supported CPU makers.
- **AI price war:** Claude Opus 5.5 was described as **40% cheaper** than Opus 5 at **$20 per million output tokens**, OpenAI GPT CS Sol at **$10**, and GPT C Luna at **$0.50**. Meta Muse and an Alibaba AI chip illustrated accelerating competition.

## Indian markets

- **Range-bound but stock-specific:** Nifty closed at **23,329** after making both a higher high and lower low. Kutumba Rao saw the underlying broader-market current weakening even as high-volume news trades appeared in individual names.
- **Options and flows:** FIIs sold nearly **Rs 4,000 crore** and domestic institutions bought more than **Rs 4,000 crore**. The rupee strengthened to about **95.62**. Calls centered at **23,500**, puts at **23,000/23,400**, and VIX stayed low at **10.99**.
- **Growth names:** Transrail Lighting rose **15-16%** after expanding annual capacity from **24,000 km to about 40,000 km**. CleanMax received a **Rs 1,700** target. Apollo Micro's management guidance was cited at at least **50%** growth this year and **100%** next year.
- **Opening breadth:** Despite weak Gift Nifty, Nifty opened up about **60 points**; BSE breadth was **1,281:665** and NSE **1,564:600**. ESDS reached a **5%** upper circuit near **Rs 1,810**, Optiemus gained **20%**, and Milky Mist **5%**.
