## Global backdrop

- **Positive US cues:** The Dow rose **0.18%**, S&P 500 **0.65%**, and Nasdaq **1.1%**; the Nasdaq 100 and Composite closed near new highs for a second day. The Dollar Index was near **102.13**.
- **Oil remains the key risk:** Crude stayed around **$100**, despite G7 releases, recovering Qatar production near **75%**, and an East-West pipeline described as **80% operational**. The show said relief required crude closer to **$90**.
- **Multi-decade yield pressure:** US yields were cited near **5.06%** for five years, **5.31%** for ten years, and **5.66%** for 30 years. France's OAT-Bund spread was around **1.59%**, evoking the old PIGS crisis, while Spain reportedly called a **November 29** snap poll.
- **Geopolitical uncertainty:** Yemen, the Houthis, Bab-el-Mandeb, Saudi support and shipping security remained prominent oil-market drivers.

## Indian markets

- **Policy-heavy week:** Consensus expected an RBI hike of **25 basis points** tomorrow. Today's Cabinet/CCEA meetings could discuss equity taxation or STT, and the GST Council meets the day after tomorrow on issues including input-tax credit. Nifty weekly expiry was also today.
- **Improving corporate updates:** Deposit growth benefited from FCNR flows, while HDFC Bank advances grew **4.9% quarter on quarter**. Kotak Mahindra Bank, Godrej Consumer, Trent, AWL Agri and Shyam Metalics gave broadly encouraging updates.
- **Breadth brought relief:** About **13 of 16 sectors** closed positive yesterday, while the opening breadth was **1,258/743** on one exchange and **1,514/807** on NSE. FIIs sold **Rs 4,699 crore**, DIIs bought more than **Rs 5,000 crore**, and the rupee weakened to **96.35**.
- **Technical map:** Kutumba Rao identified **22,800** resistance, **22,500** as the key put/caution zone, PCR recovering from about **0.7** toward **0.94-1**, and VIX near **14.7**. Ramakrishna placed the 20-day average at **23,200** and would sell rallies until a close above it.
- **IPO split:** Of **161 SME issues**, **81** traded below issue price, versus **29 of 92** mainboard issues. Jio Platforms was discussed as a possible **October 21** IPO raising **$3.88 billion / roughly Rs 36,000 crore**, with listing around **October 28**.
