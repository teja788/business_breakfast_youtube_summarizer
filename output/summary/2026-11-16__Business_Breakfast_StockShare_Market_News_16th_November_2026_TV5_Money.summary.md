## Global backdrop

- **Make-or-break AI week:** Asian markets were weak ahead of Nvidia's results and the Anthropic IPO. Nvidia lost **13% last week**, fell back toward a **$4 trillion** market value after trading above **$5 trillion**, and faced uncertainty over Jensen Huang's possible return.
- **US tech unwind:** The Dow closed positive Friday, while the Nasdaq and S&P 500 fell. Nvidia declined **2%**, AMD **8.8%**, and Broadcom **4.9%**.
- **Rates and crude:** A minimum **0.25%** Fed hike in December was expected. Crude traded near **$95**, while the IEA projected a **2 million-barrel-per-day** production surplus this quarter.

## Indian markets

- **Exceptional Q2 earnings:** Motilal Oswal's review cited **27%** year-on-year Nifty 50 PAT growth, a **17-quarter high**. Broader profit rose **32%**, EBITDA margin reached **17.5%**, small-cap profit grew **32%**, and mid-cap profit **22%**.
- **FII-led outperformance:** November inflows were cited near **Rs 79,302 crore** and potentially above **Rs 1 lakh crore** for the month. In the last month Nifty gained **9.4%**, Bank Nifty **12%**, mid-caps **8%**, small-caps **6%**, and micro-caps **8%**.
- **Key range and caution:** Nifty was expected to consolidate between **25,054 and 25,424**. Kutumba Rao warned that October retail inflation of **6.09%** could push RBI toward a **6.25%** repo rate and urged stock-specific positioning during consolidation.
- **Technical picture:** Nifty closed near **25,144**, up **157 points or 0.63%** for the week. Bank Nifty rose roughly **2,000 points or 3.71%**. Nifty RSI was **75.22**, PCR closed at **0.98** after reaching **1.47**, and VIX eased about **7%** to **15-15.5**.

## Analysts/segments

- **Kutumba Rao:** Remained bullish on India's medium-term relative strength and said an all-time high before year-end would not surprise him. He nevertheless warned about inflation, RBI policy, profit-booking and founder-dependence risk. He named speculative/positioning counters but made no explicit Buy/Add/Hold/Sell recommendation.
- **Kranthi:** Highlighted improving results at Westlife Foodworld, PTC Industries, SAFE/SE Fluid as heard, Jamna Auto and Affordable Robotic & Automation. These were earnings observations, not explicit stock recommendations.
- **Ramakrishna:** Saw a solid bullish trend with another week of consolidation possible. He cited Nifty resistance near **25,800**, heavy call interest near **25,200**, and Bank Nifty activity around **60,900-61,000**.
- **Alu Fluoride interview:** Q2 revenue was **Rs 65.2 crore**, net profit **Rs 15.2 crore**, EPS **Rs 19.38**, and the first interim dividend **Rs 2.50**. Management cited restored raw-material supply, lower logistics costs, annual price contracts and better efficiency; capacity utilization was about **70%**, capacity **24,000 tonnes**, and cash roughly **Rs 60 crore**.
