## Global backdrop

- **RBI policy dominated the morning:** The panel expected either a **25-basis-point hike** or a surprise status quo, with the tone on inflation, liquidity, crude, yields and the rupee likely to drive the session.
- **US equities ignored bond stress:** The S&P 500 and Nasdaq were near records, Nvidia's market value was cited at **$6 trillion**, and Marvell rose **6%**, even as US 10-year and 30-year yields were near **5.3%** and **5.68%**.
- **Crude and Europe remained risks:** Brent crossed **$101** after trading near **$98**, while France's OAT-Bund spread narrowed about **0.09%** but broader fiscal stress still recalled the old PIGS crisis.
- **AI spending stayed the bull case:** AMD discussed a chip market of roughly **$70-90 billion**, reinforcing expectations that elevated AI-chip demand could persist for years.

## Indian markets

- **Downtrend despite tradable rallies:** Nifty remained about **500 points** below recent highs, while small caps bounced roughly **2.5%** during the week. The show urged continued SIPs in smaller companies with strong earnings rather than treating every **150-300 point** Nifty rally as a durable bottom.
- **FII selling eased but continued:** The cited sequence fell from **Rs 10,148 crore** to **Rs 9,400 crore**, **Rs 4,700 crore** and **Rs 2,960 crore**. DIIs bought about **Rs 5,000 crore** in the latest session.
- **Policy/technical map:** Kutumba Rao placed resistance at **23,000-23,100**, put support near **22,500-22,700**, PCR in the bulls' favour, and VIX at **13.6** after **14.5**. Ramakrishna required a close above the **23,092/23,100** 20-day average before calling the market buy-on-dips.
- **Growth remained resilient:** The World Bank lifted its India forecast from **6.5%** to about **7-7.1%**. Expected Nifty-company revenue and profit growth were cited near **20%** and **22-23%**.
- **Opening was weak:** Nifty opened down **160 points**, Sensex **420** and Bank Nifty **332**, with breadth near **1:2**, although selected small and mid caps outperformed.

## Analysts/segments

- **Kutumba Rao:** Said the RBI outcome looked largely discounted, though cash-market volatility and external risks required observation. He highlighted system liquidity falling from about **Rs 12 lakh crore** to **Rs 4-6 lakh crore**, power prices doubling, thermal-coal cover of only **7-10 days**, and a results season that should become extremely stock-specific.
- **Ramakrishna:** Treated the two-day move as a pullback rather than a confirmed reversal. Nifty had recovered about **350 points** and Bank Nifty **1,500**, but he recommended caution, hedges and stop-losses below the 20-day average.
- **Kranthi:** Expected Navi Mumbai airport's airline transition to smooth out over weeks or months, stressed the economy's underlying strength, and said PTC Industries' discounted QIP appeared linked to capex plus an acquisition; the participants should be watched.
- **Stock answers:** Kutumba Rao advised holding Natco Pharma for at least another **six months**, with potential to return above four figures. On Waaree Energies he cited margin compression and US tariffs, and treated BESS as a preliminary opportunity to watch until results indicate whether averaging makes sense.
