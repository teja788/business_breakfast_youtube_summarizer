## Global backdrop

- **Risk remained elevated after a global sell-off:** Asian markets were mostly lower, US AI/chip stocks sold off, and VIX rose more than **10%** to about **15.275**. Gift Nifty and US futures nevertheless pointed to a positive opening.
- **Crude stayed above $100:** Brent reached roughly **$104** before easing to above **$103** after Donald Trump said there would be no attacks until **November 3** and talks with Iran were under way.
- **OpenAI expectations hit AI sentiment:** Annualised revenue was cited near **$50 billion** versus an expected **$70 billion**, a **$20 billion** gap, while a future valuation above **$1 trillion** was discussed.
- **Global alternatives competed for capital:** Kranthi said foreign funds can choose companies such as TSMC with multifold profit visibility, whereas many Indian companies struggle to guide for **10-15%** growth.

## Indian markets

- **Broad panic erased wealth:** Nifty fell more than **400 points** intraday, roughly **Rs 10 lakh crore** of market value was wiped out, and breadth deteriorated to about **1:3 or 1:4**. Mid caps lost about **2.4%** and small caps roughly **2-2.2%**.
- **GQG block sale amplified rumours:** GQG sold about **Rs 9,500 crore** of ITC in a block deal. It was also cited as having sold around **Rs 12,000 crore** of Adani shares and **Rs 28,000 crore** of Indian stocks over the past year, largely amid redemption pressure.
- **Institutional and currency pressure persisted:** Total FII selling was cited at **Rs 12,944 crore**, while the rupee weakened to about **96.88** per dollar. Excluding the ITC block, domestic buying was said to be only around **Rs 3,000-4,000 crore**.
- **Technical map stayed fragile:** **44 of 50** Nifty stocks were below their 200-day averages and **10** were near 52-week lows. Kutumba Rao placed medium-term support at **22,000**, resistance around **22,350-22,400**, call concentration at **22,500** and **22,700**, and PCR near **0.73**.
- **Results offered a counterweight:** TCS international business grew **1.2%**, AI reached **10%** of revenue, EBIT margin was about **24.2%**, and second-quarter deal wins were around **$10 billion**. Nifty 50 Q2 earnings growth was estimated at **24.7%**, potentially the best in **12 quarters**.
- **Opening recovery moderated:** Nifty opened about **125 points** higher, Sensex **360** and Bank Nifty **275**, but Nifty's gain had eased to roughly **80 points** by the close of the programme.

## Analysts/segments

- **Kutumba Rao:** Expected **22,000** to hold in the medium term but said the market could weaken further if it lost that level. VIX above **15** favoured bears, while PCR near **0.73** could tempt contrarian positional buying. He preferred defensive pharma, active metals and precision engineering and urged patient investors to replace weak holdings with quality names.
- **Kranthi:** Attributed GQG's reductions to global portfolio reallocation and redemptions rather than a rejection of India. For power equipment, he favoured buying quality companies only on declines, without chasing.
- **Ramakrishna:** Said a ninth consecutive losing week was possible, with Nifty about **16.47%** below its all-time high. A previous short below **22,500** had led to a roughly **320-point** fall; for the day he avoided an index trade and suggested Coforge around **Rs 1,860-1,875** with a **Rs 1,750** stop-loss.
- **GST reforms:** The prosecution threshold was raised from **Rs 1 crore to Rs 5 crore**; claims below **Rs 10,000** would avoid demand notices, and **Rs 10,000-25,000** cases would face only a general penalty. Accumulated plant-and-machinery ITC refunds were made available from **April 1, 2022**.
