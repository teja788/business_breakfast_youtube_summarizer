## Global backdrop

- **Oil/war:** Spot crude traded around **$106.44**, though the next-month December contract was near **$98**. Strait of Hormuz flows remained about **5-6 million barrels/day** below pre-war levels but had not stopped, while Saudi exports rose sharply in September.
- **US yields/trade:** The US **30-year yield reached 5.53%**, its highest since June 2004; the ten-year was about **4.85%**. The US-China trade truce was extended by two months, with tariff decisions deferred to January.
- **Iran uncertainty:** Both the US and Iran continued confrontational statements while acknowledging indirect diplomacy. A conclusive deal was seen as the likely trigger for a short-term equity rally.

## Indian markets

- **Seven-week decline:** India underperformed other BRICS markets, with Nifty near **23,100**, down **6.2% in 12 months** and about **12% over two years**. Kutumba Rao and Ramakrishna both treated **23,000** as critical; a sustained break could expose **22,200** or another **400-500 points** of downside.
- **Large/small divergence:** The smallcap index gained **29.67% in six months**, while select metals returned about **30%** and pharma pockets **16-18%** over two years. Better balance sheets supported the move: the proportion of companies with interest coverage below one for three years fell from **23.5% to 14.2%**.
- **Opening selloff:** Nifty's loss widened from **130** to **200-220 points**; Sensex and Bank Nifty were each down roughly **670 points** by the end of the programme. Nearly all sector indices were negative.
- **NSE/expiry:** NSE slipped to about **Rs 1,773**, below its **Rs 1,785** issue price. Monthly options expiry was due the next day in a four-session week.
- **Capstone bulk deal:** Nandamuri Kalyan Ram and family bought **10 lakh shares** after the promoter sold **12 lakh**. The show cited profit CAGR of **29%**, sales CAGR of **27%**, five-year stock CAGR of **80%**, and an IPO holding growing from **1,200 to 4,200 shares** and about **Rs 1 lakh to Rs 22.68 lakh**.

## Analysts/segments

- **Kutumba Rao:** Said the macro picture was cloudy but company-level opportunities remained. He warned against speculative volume-led moves, allowed a courageous current-level Turtlemint buy, advised partial profit-taking in Yatharth, preferred SCTL near **Rs 390** and ideally **Rs 360**, recommended switching Bank of Baroda to SBI and Lemon Tree to Indian Hotels, and backed Capstone for one year.
- **Kranthi:** Said Hyderabad companies had improved governance and generated strong returns. He advised adding Raymond only on dips after results, booking partial profit in Syrma SGS, and holding Piramal Pharma for a possible **Rs 280-290**.
- **Ramakrishna:** Put the next major support after **23,000** near **22,200**, noted Nifty was about **3%** below its 50-day average and **4-5%** below its 100/200-day averages, and advised waiting for a bounce toward **23,030-23,040** before considering another short.
