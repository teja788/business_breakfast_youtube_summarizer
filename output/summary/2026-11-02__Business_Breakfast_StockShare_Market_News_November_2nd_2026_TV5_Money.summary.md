## Global backdrop

- **US technology sold off:** Nasdaq fell almost **2.85%** on Friday, while Nasdaq futures recovered **0.8%**. The panel cited an AI/semiconductor rout including declines of **29%**, **11%**, **4.2%**, and **7.2%** in the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index.
- **AI capex and debt drew scrutiny:** Estimated AI-company capex was cited at **$2.55 trillion** this year, while Kutumba Rao put borrowings by four large companies at about **$700 billion**, comparable with the economy of Argentina or Belgium.
- **Indian IT could benefit from an anti-AI rotation:** Accenture rose **4.5%** and Cognizant **4%**, while Adobe and the Infosys/Wipro ADRs closed positive.
- **Crude eased and US politics mattered:** Oil fell from about **$118** to **$111** after a diplomatic statement from Donald Trump. Kutumba Rao said the **November 3** US midterms could swing global markets, especially if Democrats won both Houses.

## Indian markets

- **Domestic macro data stayed strong:** October GST collections were cited at **Rs 2.31 lakh crore**, up **23%** and the second-highest in four years. UPI averaged **Rs 1 lakh crore per day**, total monthly value exceeded **Rs 31 lakh crore**, and bank-credit growth was **17.9%** by mid-October with a possible **19%** month-end rate.
- **The market broke above its recent band:** Kutumba Rao said the **22,500-23,000** range had been left behind; defending **23,000** could keep **23,500** on track. PCR was near **1.4**, VIX around **15**, and his broad message was to remain invested.
- **Opening breadth was strongly positive:** Nifty rose more than **200 points**, Sensex **750**, and Bank Nifty **268**. Reliance gained about **4.52-4.6%**, while Motherson Sumi Wiring rose **12.27%** and BEL **1.2%**.
- **Laurus outlined long-term expansion:** Management cited recruitment of **3,000** people and **Rs 3,000 crore** capex through its difficult period, a new **Rs 300 crore** ADC facility, about **Rs 8,000 crore** capex over five years, and planned Unit 7 inauguration around April.
- **Sai Parenterals reported margin expansion:** Q2 revenue was **Rs 196 crore**, gross profit **Rs 93 crore**, gross margin **47.5%**, EBITDA margin **21.1%**, PAT margin **5.4%**, and PAT **Rs 11 crore**. H1 revenue was about **Rs 376 crore**, PAT **Rs 18-18.4 crore**, and EBITDA margin **17.6%**.

## Analysts/segments

- **Kutumba Rao:** Said India's **7.8-8%** GDP reading, earnings and FII buying after nearly **nine weeks** supported the market, while CPI near **5.54**, food inflation near **7.74**, West Asia and US elections were risks.
- **Sai Parenterals strategy:** Management expects revenue to be well above the formal **Rs 750 crore** guidance, targets gross margin above **45%**, and said investment of about **Rs 600 crore** and higher depreciation will weigh on PAT for another **three to four quarters**.
- **Capacity and CDMO:** Unit 3 capacity is due by **November 30**, rising from **85.5 million** to **155 million units per month**. Sai Prateek has **86 molecules** under development and access to **279** previously developed molecules; four specialized molecules are due for disclosure within **one week to 10 days**.
- **Kutumba Rao stock calls:** Long-term Laurus Labs investors were told there was no need to sell despite a normal **4-5%** post-result correction. An E2E Networks holder at **Rs 690** was advised to wait patiently; minor support was near **Rs 460** and major support **Rs 397-425**.
- **Other analysts:** Ramakrishna suggested considering a Nifty long near **23,200** after a first-hour cooling-off. A technical analyst described Sai Parenterals as strengthened and near a short-term breakout, but the captions did not make that speaker's identity explicit.
